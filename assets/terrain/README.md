# Original painted terrain materials

These are original rendering assets made with the built-in imagegen tool; they do not contain game-pack exports. Rust embeds them for native and browser parity. The October 20 granite material supplies outdoor mountain faces, caps and exposed mineral beneath grassy banks. The October 10 bedrock supplies other stone walls, including Union Cave and the desaturated rock beneath Ice Path's blue frost. The earlier sandstone remains available as source artwork. Map-space projection is defined in `crates/crystal-voxel-view/src/voxel_surface.wesl`.

## Granite prompt — 2026-10-20

File: `painted-granite.png`, 1254 × 1254, opaque RGB. Used with triplanar projection, filtered mip levels and the existing texture binding. This candidate supplies fractured surface detail; the outdoor shader restores warm ochre pigment guided by the original game's feel. The prompt below records the asset's generation, not a replacement for the game's palette and mood.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a square seamlessly tileable albedo texture for triplanar projection onto 3D outdoor mountain cliffs in a polished stylized adventure game. Create the flat 2D material texture only, not a scene or render of a rock. Hand-painted broad angular fractured granite plates, irregular intersecting diagonal and vertical fissures, bevel-like quiet painted edge wear, several large restful faces with smaller broken plates only in the recesses. Warm neutral limestone/taupe with muted sage-gray mineral variation and subtle pale warm edges, narrow charcoal-brown cracks. Sophisticated clean painterly 3D game art with deliberate large shapes, moderate contrast, very sparse fine grain. Exactly orthographic straight-on texture, uniform diffuse illumination, no overall light direction and no cast shadows. Every edge must tile seamlessly in both axes. Avoid round cobblestones, regular brick grids, thin repeated horizontal sediment stripes, piled individual rocks, moss, plants, landscape, perspective, borders, text, labels and watermarks. 1024 by 1024 square; opaque surface edge to edge.

## Sandstone prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production game material asset: one square seamless tileable hand-painted sandstone/earth cliff albedo texture, viewed straight on orthographically with no perspective. Rich ochre sandstone, warm tawny browns, muted golden stone highlights, cool brown crevices; restrained saturation. Broad irregular eroded sedimentary bands with angular chipped facets and occasional thin dark mineral fissures; painterly soft brushwork and crisp selectively painted edges like an illustrated lush Pokemon forest environment. Material must read as stone rather than cloudy noise, camouflage, bricks or wood. Continuous irregular horizontal strata, never a regular grid or repeating square blocks. Fill the entire image with the material. Flat diffuse ambient illumination, no directional lighting, no cast shadows, no ambient occlusion, no bevel around image, no objects, no ground, no vegetation, no text, no border. Seamless opposing edges for tiling. High-quality 1024x1024 albedo asset.

Broad irregular interlocking angular stone plates and chipped edges, varied non-repeating polygon sizes, occasional thin branching charcoal cracks, small eroded chips. Warm muted grey-brown stone, subtle ochre mineral inclusions, soft dusty pale grey highlights and cool dark umber fissures. Painterly brushwork suited to a lush illustrated Pokemon forest game, rich readable medium-scale forms. No horizontal sedimentary stripes, no wood grain, no cloudy noise, no pebble mosaic, no regular grid, no bricks.
