# Electabuzz and Elekid source review

The [official Electabuzz](https://sg.portal-pokemon.com/pokedex/0125/) and
[official Elekid](https://sg.portal-pokemon.com/pokedex/0239/) pages returned
200. Their named primary artworks were inspected in separate background
image-only tabs, which were then closed. No reference media was copied into
the repository.

Electabuzz has a substantial yellow body, broad low head, sharp ivory eyes,
a black forehead chevron, twin pointed scalp tufts, yellow stalks with gold
terminal balls, an open fang-bearing jaw, large chest bolt and flank stripes,
striped bent arms with clenched hands and wrist fur, striped shins, broad feet
with paired front ivory toe claws and rear heel claws, and a long striped tail with a rounded end.
Elekid has one continuous yellow egg silhouette, two yellow contact blades
with genuine upper holes, angular eyes and a small pointed grin, a lower
body bolt and black waist wrap, slim arm roots leading to broad two-striped
forearms and clawed fists, and short yellow legs and feet. Its blade tips are
yellow, not the old black caps.

The baseline paired software oblique render exited zero and was inspected.
Both have the same rounded head/torso, protruding eye spheres, oversized simple
fists and unsupported planar stripe triangles. Electabuzz lacks its muzzle,
crest, claws and actual striped tail. Elekid lacks the egg silhouette and
uses black contact caps without actual holes.

The specific [heaveninvoid_ Elekid Line metadata](https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/1cf03f17b8124102b31ee6f531b609ea)
returned 200, not-downloadable status, 2,376 faces, zero listed clips and an
empty license object. Its description identifies Elekid/Electabuzz/Electivire
models for Cobblemon and credits subsequent improvements by JoeSeff. This
listing does not provide an accessible openly licensed archive for import.

The specific [ToadKnight Electabuzz metadata](https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/170ed7ec5bc740baaab1dc95c531976e)
returned 200, not-downloadable status, 48,216 faces, zero listed clips, an empty
description and an empty license object. No open archive was exposed.

No meshes, skin, bones or animation were retrieved or imported. Zero listed
clips alone does not prove that an asset has no rig. These checks cover
specific listings and do not prove that no usable open rig exists. The local
replacements are original static geometry.

The [static Black & White back sprite](https://img.pokemondb.net/sprites/black-white/back-normal/electabuzz.png)
was also visually inspected in a background image-only tab through the
[Pokémon Database gallery](https://pokemondb.net/sprites/electabuzz). It shows
a thick curved vertical dorsal stripe. The original rear patch follows that
marking; no sprite was downloaded or included in the bundle.
