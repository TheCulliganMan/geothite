# Magmar and Magby source review

The [official Magmar](https://sg.portal-pokemon.com/pokedex/0126/) and
[official Magby](https://sg.portal-pokemon.com/pokedex/0240/) pages returned
200. Their named primary artworks were individually inspected in background
image-only browser tabs. No reference pixels were copied into the repository.

Magmar has an orange body, a broad yellow belly with upward flame-shaped
edges, twin orange forehead lobes topped by flowing yellow fire, narrow ivory
eyes, an open cream duck bill and pink tongue. Dark blue bands wrap the neck
and lower legs. Rounded yellow shoulders and thighs meet orange arms with
jagged cuff structures, ivory hand claws and broad orange two-clawed feet.
Pointed orange dorsal structures and a long yellow tail with a yellow/orange
flame distinguish the back silhouette.

Magby has a small red pear body and a pointed yellow belly patch, broad red
head with a rounded cloud-like multi-lobed crown, tall fitted ivory eyes and
small vertical pupils, a yellow bill with a rounded mouth opening, and a dark
neck collar. Long red arms end in three ivory claws; slim red shins meet
small two-clawed feet. Its rear tail is red and pointed, without a flame.

The paired baseline software render exited zero and was fully inspected.
Both reuse the same simple rounded head and body, oversized protruding eye
spheres, single oval bill, raised oval belly and undifferentiated mitten hands.
Neither has the dark collar, clawed hands or toes, or fitted eye anatomy.
Magmar uses sharp floating fire pyramids rather than flowing twin crown fires.
Magby has only three detached-looking cap balls and incorrectly has a flaming
tail. These are not final-quality replacements.
