# Hitmon family source review

The [official Hitmonlee artwork](https://sg.portal-pokemon.com/pokedex/0106/)
was inspected in a background image-only browser tab, then the tab was closed.
Its continuous headless tan body, sloping black eyes with small white pupils,
cream segmented shins and wrists, three tan fingers per hand, three ivory toe
claws per foot and round gold sole marks distinguish it from the other three
fighters. A software oblique render of the current baseline exited zero and
was individually inspected: it has a separate rounded upper body, protruding
white/black eyeballs, raised spherical fists and brown ring bands around simple
tan leg rods.

The [nguyenlouis32 Hitmonlee metadata](https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/b7534fa6f08e4760839df0f80f00e072)
reports downloadable CC BY 4.0 geometry, 4,814 faces and zero animation clips.
Its description supplies character lore without authoring provenance. The
download endpoint returned HTTP 401. Neither mesh nor skeleton was retrieved;
zero clips alone does not establish whether bones exist.

The [Iván Arévalo Hitmonchan metadata](https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/b831399546514e8cb28cbbfddb968bfe)
reports downloadable CC BY 4.0 geometry, 1,738,698 faces and zero animation clips.
Its primary description identifies original Blender sculpting and vertex
painting. The download endpoint returned HTTP 401; no mesh or skeleton was
retrieved or imported.

The [Laxminarayan Artistry animated pack](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/low-poly-animated-pokemon-cartoon-character-pack-096fd912250e44d388bf7f3fb49c3a7a)
explicitly includes Hitmonchan and advertises Mixamo animation in a Blender
file. Its [metadata](https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/096fd912250e44d388bf7f3fb49c3a7a)
reports downloadable CC BY 4.0 geometry, 113,257 faces and one animation clip.
The download endpoint returned HTTP 401. The creator's linked
[website](https://sites.google.com/view/laxminarayanartistry) and
[shop](https://sites.google.com/view/laxminarayanartistry/shop) were read;
their displayed free products do not supply this pack. No mesh, skin or bones
were retrieved. This is an advertised animated candidate whose actual access
and compatibility remain unverified, not an imported usable open rig.

The [3DS Pokémon X/Y Tyrogue metadata](https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/4810548170f04c0c890944a7b960c3ed)
reports downloadable CC BY 4.0 geometry, 4,252 faces, zero clips and an empty
description. The title identifies a game asset and does not establish original
authorship. The download endpoint returned HTTP 401; no geometry or skeleton
was retrieved or imported.

These checks cover specific candidates rather than proving no open rig exists.
The Hitmonlee replacement is original static geometry with authored continuous
spring surfaces; it does not claim a borrowed skeleton or animation.

The [official Hitmonchan artwork](https://sg.portal-pokemon.com/pokedex/0107/)
was subsequently inspected in a separate background image-only tab, then the
tab was closed. Its continuous five-point tan crown/skull, pointed tan shoulder
guards, angular blue gaze, lavender sleeveless tunic and pleated skirt, dark
waist belt, red padded mitts with thumbs and lavender boots are distinct from
Hitmonlee. The baseline software oblique render exited zero and was inspected:
it has detached crown spikes, protruding spherical eyes, exposed tan torso,
simple red hand spheres, disconnected purple hem leaves and tan feet.

The [official Hitmontop artwork](https://sg.portal-pokemon.com/pokedex/0237/)
was inspected in a background image-only tab, then closed. It shows the
handstand on a pointed tan helmet, broad rounded head, black round eyes with
ivory pupils, a compact blue body with tan belly shield, long tan limbs,
ivory wrist/ankle wraps, round fists, large blue feet with three ivory spikes
and tan sole pads, and a curved tail ending in a smaller blue ball and point.
The baseline software oblique render exited zero and was inspected: its generic
upright brown fighter lacks those defining blue balance ends and tail.

The specific [nguyenlouis32 Hitmontop metadata](https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/2b4e22929ef548aaaf745cbe0807bcc7)
reports downloadable CC BY 4.0 geometry with 1,574 faces and zero clips.
Its description gives character lore without authoring provenance. The
model's download endpoint returned HTTP 401. No mesh, bones or skin were
retrieved; zero listed clips alone does not prove the asset has no rig.
The replacement is original static geometry, with no external imported rig.

The [official Tyrogue artwork](https://sg.portal-pokemon.com/pokedex/0236/)
was inspected in a background image-only tab, then the tab was closed. Its
continuous three-pronged lilac scalp, rounded jaw, oval side ears, angular
ivory/gold eyes, compact body, cream waist and wrist wraps, brown shorts,
open three-finger hands, slim lilac legs and brown shoes differ from the
adult fighters. The baseline software oblique image exited zero and was
inspected: detached scalp spikes, spherical protruding eyes, oversized fists,
a bulky torso and lilac shoes omit those defining features.

The same specific Tyrogue metadata endpoint was checked again: it returned
200 with downloadable CC BY 4.0 status, 4,252 faces, zero listed clips and an
empty description. The title still identifies a 3DS Pokémon X/Y asset;
original authoring provenance is unestablished. Its download endpoint again
returned 401. No archive or rig was retrieved or imported, and zero listed
clips does not by itself prove a model lacks bones.
