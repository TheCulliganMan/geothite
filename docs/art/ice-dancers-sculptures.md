its existing static JSON asset. Smoochum's canonical asset is now

insets, broad purple face, recessed oval kiss rim, long blonde hair, bent white
sleeves and five-finger purple palms. All 45 parts' geometry and colors regenerate

with three-finger hands, and planted toes. Its broad face has fitted white eyes,
dark pupils, lower irises and glints. A short golden bob has curved side locks,
a notched fringe and four rooted leaf-like tufts. The head and shortened throat
retain their existing proportions. Local pack normal colors are purple-pink
(165,8,156), gold (239,198,33), white and black, including the gold kiss rim.

mouth patches recessed behind the open centers. Stored-geometry checks require
root volume, an unfilled center and actual depth, and detect a filled negative.
Smoochum's fitted gaze, kiss rim and mouth share the head joint, preserving their
relative geometry through all clips.
