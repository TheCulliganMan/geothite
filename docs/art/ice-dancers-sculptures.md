# Original Jynx and Smoochum sculptures

The [October 20 depth and posture audit](ice-fighter-fidelity.md) supersedes the
earlier proportion acceptance. `tools/ice_dancers_sculpt.py` authors both
original models. `tools/jynx_glb.py` and `tools/smoochum_glb.py` export and
strictly read their canonical GLBs; neither uses static JSON at runtime.

Smoochum's large rounded head and deep bob sit over a compact body, short
cream supports and tiny feet. A continuous rounded crown backs its swept
notched fringe at both temples. Curved side locks and four leaves complete the
bob. The fitted gaze uses the pack's white, black and purple; horizontal gold
lips surround a real depression in the closed head. The white collar, fitted
bib, small arms and three-finger hands occupy the revised proportions.

Eleven joints control torso, neck, head, side locks, tuft bank, arms and leg
middles. Toes are fixed in source clips. Face, lips and fringe share the head;
each hand and all its digits share its arm. Neutral-ended idle/action/recoil
clips preserve occupied joins. The asset has 38 parts, 17,939 vertices, 30,344
triangles, 191 accessors and 759,748 bytes. Source geometry pin:
`a889f184acd49b5a2277db754a7452861aca2634a19629f37e777db0b0cc1b68`.
Authoring height stays 0.78; pack height `104` supplies 0.4064 m. Normal colors
remain purple (165,8,156), gold (239,198,33), white and black.

Jynx's broader seven-fold bell skirt, fuller bodice, revised face and swept
hair support the distinct Crystal hand positions. Its ivory hair and trim,
pink gown/lips and plum skin preserve the local normal palette. Both sleeves,
all ten fingers, layered tips and kiss rim retain real roots. The
[Jynx skin guide](jynx-skin.md) records its 22-control rig and logical hand packing.

The shared 11 anatomy checks, seven independent fidelity tests, seven Smoochum
skin checks and nine Jynx skin checks cover actual stored surfaces and sampled
posed joins. The audit records byte-identical Blender regeneration, full neutral
orbits, sampled motion, production coverage and its limits. Reference-only
models and textures never become authoring inputs. Nothing is committed.
