# Jynx and Smoochum source review

This is a historical front-art review. The [October 20 full-volume audit](ice-fighter-fidelity.md)
supersedes its visual acceptance and static-asset description with inspected
front/back sprites, both profiles, genuine game orbits and revised canonical skins.

The [official Jynx](https://sg.portal-pokemon.com/pokedex/0124/) and
[official Smoochum](https://sg.portal-pokemon.com/pokedex/0238/) pages returned
200. Their named primary artworks were inspected in separate background
image-only browser tabs, which were then closed. No reference media was copied
into the repository.

Jynx has a broad purple face, round white eyes with tiny dark pupils, an oval
pink kiss rim, long parted blonde hair, a red flowing gown with gold chest
trim and red insets, white sleeves and open purple five-finger hands. Smoochum
has a large rounded pink face, broad white/green/dark eyes, an oval pink kiss
rim, a short blonde bob with notched fringe and a four-leaf tuft, a compact
pink lower body, a cream chest and short cream arms, hands and feet.

The baseline paired software oblique render exited zero and was inspected.
Both use a coarse cap and side hair lobes, protruding eye spheres, oversized
fists and purple torsos/feet. Jynx has disconnected red hem leaves instead of
a complete gown. Smoochum lacks the cream chest/limbs and green eyes.

The specific [nguyenlouis32 Jynx metadata](https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/e74c3beb0660497c8398959652a263b5)
returned 200 with downloadable CC BY 4.0 status, 5,434 faces and zero listed
animation clips. The description provides character lore without original
authoring provenance. Its download endpoint returned 401.

The specific [nguyenlouis32 Smoochum metadata](https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/26ed973b2e494339ac5ea3d1cf7e801c)
returned 200 with downloadable CC BY 4.0 status, 1,554 faces and zero listed
animation clips. Its description also gives character lore without authoring
provenance. Its download endpoint returned 401.

No geometry, skin, bones or animations were retrieved or imported. Zero listed
clips alone does not establish that a model lacks a skeleton. These checks
cover specific candidates and do not prove that no usable open rig exists.
The local replacements are original static geometry.
