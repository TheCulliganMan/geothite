# Nidoran family source review

The [official Nidorina artwork](https://sg.portal-pokemon.com/pokedex/0030/)
was inspected in a background image-only browser tab. Its broad hornless head,
orange pointed gaze, notched blue ears with navy interiors, small ivory fangs,
pale breast, substantial spotted hindquarters, downturned forepaws, ivory
claws, short pointed tail and swept dorsal barbs distinguish it from the other
five family members. The installed baseline was rendered and individually
inspected in a software oblique view: it has a forehead horn, protruding
spherical eyes, simple leaf ears, a generic rounded body and plain feet.

The [nguyenlouis32 Nidoran male metadata](https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/b475e6fd75334ffb84c68bf37206da1d)
reports downloadable CC BY 4.0 geometry, 4,616 faces and zero animation clips.
The description supplies character lore without establishing authoring
provenance. The download endpoint returned HTTP 401. No mesh or skeleton was
retrieved; zero clips alone does not establish whether bones exist.

The [Nidorina metadata](https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/6a1db524a020431c877542d48b5c741f)
reports 35,904 faces, zero animation clips, `isDownloadable: false`, an empty
description and no license. Its download endpoint returned HTTP 401. No mesh
or skeleton was retrieved or imported.

The [3DS Pokémon X/Y Nidoking metadata](https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/58a4cec0b20c4a43821f22946bf01e4b)
reports downloadable CC BY 4.0 geometry, 5,714 faces, zero animation clips and
an empty description. The title explicitly identifies a game asset; the
listing does not establish original authorship. Its download endpoint returned
HTTP 401. No geometry or skeleton was retrieved or imported.

The [free Free3D Nidorina listing](https://free3d.com/3d-model/nidorina-pokemon-11773.html)
explicitly specifies a Personal Use License, Rigged No and Animated No. The
[Simon Telezhkin Nidorina listing](https://free3d.com/3d-model/pokemon-nidorina-lowpoly-pbr-rigged-plus-walkcycle-6440.html)
advertises a rig and baked walk cycle, costs $2 and labels the license Editorial
Only. Neither provides an open rig suitable for import here. Neither was
downloaded or purchased.

These are checks of specific candidates, not an exhaustive claim that no open
rig exists. The [new original static Nidorina](nidorina-sculpture.md) has replaced
the installed model after geometry tests and three-angle software inspection.
Its individually inspected muted native GPU capture and all-251 Rust model
regression have passed, with its current source fingerprints verified in both
executables.

The [official Nidorino artwork](https://sg.portal-pokemon.com/pokedex/0033/)
was subsequently inspected in a separate background image-only tab. Nidorino
has a low four-legged posture, long lowered wedge skull, forward nasal lance,
particularly tall ragged ears with teal interiors, pointed narrow eyes, small
fangs, irregular dark purple head/flank/leg markings, upright dorsal barbs,
ivory paw claws and a short rear point. The installed baseline was rendered
and individually inspected: it is a purple variant of the rounded generic
family body, with protruding eyes, rose ear interiors and plain feet.

The [ThirdSon Nidorino listing](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/nidorino-d679382592e340ae9c1be6bf29e7f12f)
explicitly describes anatomical modeling practice and creation of a rig. Its
[metadata](https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/d679382592e340ae9c1be6bf29e7f12f)
reports downloadable CC BY 4.0 geometry, 32,420 faces and one animation. It was
found in the curated animated-model collection results and its primary listing was
read directly. The download endpoint returned HTTP 401. The creator's linked
[Maya scene](https://drive.google.com/open?id=10g0Lu3pwZMrur99l7yZlyNGCuHTHzbe-)
returned HTTP 404 when fetched directly; the web reader also could not access
it. Neither mesh nor skeleton was retrieved. This candidate advertises a rig
and an open license, but access and actual skin/bone compatibility remain
unverified. It is not an imported usable rig.

The [official female Nidoran artwork](https://sg.portal-pokemon.com/pokedex/0029/)
and [official male Nidoran artwork](https://sg.portal-pokemon.com/pokedex/0032/)
were individually inspected in background image-only tabs. The female has
pale periwinkle skin, a compact tall rounded body, broad outward ears with navy
fields and teal lower lobes, a small upright horn, orange eyes, cheek barbs and
dark flank markings. The male has a low crouching purple body, pronounced
hindquarters, much larger upright ragged teal-lined ears, a forward horn,
pointed orange gaze and dorsal barbs. A software oblique sheet of both installed
baselines exited zero and was inspected: both retain the same generic rounded
body, protruding eye spheres, small simple leaf ears and plain feet. Male ear
interiors and flank dots are incorrectly rose-colored in that recipe.

The Nidoran male metadata and failed download are recorded above. A focused
search for rigged female/male Nidoran did not yield a stronger directly
accessible rig candidate; this does not prove that no such rig exists.

The [official Nidoqueen artwork](https://sg.portal-pokemon.com/pokedex/0031/)
and [official Nidoking artwork](https://sg.portal-pokemon.com/pokedex/0034/)
were individually inspected in background image-only tabs. Nidoqueen has blue
scale armor, a small broad reptilian head, short horn, broad round brown-lined
ears, separate pale chest plates, a pale segmented lower belly, shoulder and
knee armor, strong clawed limbs and a long substantial blue tail. Nidoking has
purple scale armor, a lowered wedge skull, larger forward horn, ragged teal-lined
ears, ivory chest/belly armor, prominent dorsal barbs, broad claws and a long
upturned segmented tail. The baseline oblique sheet exited zero and was
inspected: both are simple variants of the same upright rounded body with
protruding eyes, leaf ears, oversized unsegmented belly patches and plain feet.

The [Nidoqueen metadata](https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/f28e0bff333b44c7902658c1975def14)
found in the curated collection reports downloadable CC BY 4.0 geometry, 4,986
faces and zero animation clips. Its description supplies character lore without
an authoring source. The download endpoint returned HTTP 401. No mesh or bones
were retrieved or imported.

The [Nidoking idle-animation metadata](https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/2217efa9d30f4f7a9b4039583f07a6b6)
reports 5,714 faces, one animation, `isDownloadable: false` and a null license.
Its description discusses an early animation without supplying an open asset.
No geometry or skeleton was retrieved or imported. The previously checked
downloadable 3DS Nidoking entry is recorded above. These checks do not establish
an accessible validated open rig for either adult.
