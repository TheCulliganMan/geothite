# Rattata sculpture and standard skin

Rattata uses one canonical authored asset,
`crates/crystal-voxel-view/models/battle_species/rattata.glb`, generated by
`tools/rattata_sculpt.py` and `tools/rattata_glb.py`. The local content pack's
front/back sprites supplied the reference. No game art or extracted content is
stored alongside the model.

The low purple quadruped has a cream underside and muzzle, two separate flat
incisors, six thin whiskers, round ears with fitted pink bowls, alert fitted
red eyes, four planted paws with connected three-lobed toe ridges, and a raised
curled tail. The final body material is brighter purple after production
comparison with the source sprite. The neutral silhouette is floor rooted and
0.82 authored units tall; the pack's one-foot height scales it to 0.3048 m in
battle. Overworld props share the exact neutral mesh through the publisher's
verified Rattey identity; the NPC's source dialogue, cry and sprite stay intact.

The GLB has 42 parts, 11,494 vertices, 20,432 triangles and 569,804 bytes. Its
neutral geometry/material digest is
`ce7c14e7cf1694a4131d3ed3e3c709fe51f9b2ff7618089d3c40bf731aeb8a3d`.
There is no parallel JSON geometry source or generic fox substitution.

Nine standard skin joints form the floor root, torso, neck, head, two ears,
and a three-joint curled tail. Paws, toe ridges and lower legs remain on the
root. Face details, muzzle and incisors belong to the head; ear bowls share
the ear bones. Tail weights follow the curve's arc order, including the
returning curl, rather than assuming a monotonic rear coordinate.

The rotation-only clips are idle (3.4 seconds, 85 keys), attack and hit
(one second, 65 keys each). The torso, neck and head brace early; the tail
settles later. Source battle cues still own combat timing and effects.
The rig reader keeps its existing byte, vertex, accessor and animation bounds.

Seven Python checks cover canonical bytes, connected closed solids, shading,
rodent anatomy, fitted ear bowls and face ownership, inverse binds, planted
paws, floor clearance, dense clip samples, winding and strain. The Rust
registry/neutral reuse and nine-joint GPU activation checks pass. Broader rig,
skinning and actor regressions are recorded in the animated-models notes.

The final brighter material was inspected in paired native battle and Viridian
3D/2D views. Both captures preserved state: battle hash 567313881, LCD frame
36, virtual time 0.908207625; Viridian hash 2556627940, LCD frame 40, virtual
time 0.935438249. Rattata stays small beside Sudowoodo at its packed height;
its teeth and tail remain readable. The neutral NPC replaces Rattey's former
sprite card. Native colour is closer to the source battle purple after the
material adjustment, with intentional shaded volume rather than sprite highlights.
Browser rendering and detailed source-cue visual motion remain open.

The attempted trainer preview (`--enemy-gust --species RATTATA`) failed during
runtime snapshot save-reference validation: its Vance trainer carries
`EVENT_BEAT_BIRD_KEEPER_VANCE`, while the compiled `TrainerBirdKeeperVance1`
entry has an empty event flag. The supported `--wild-species RATTATA` preview
passed and supplied the battle comparison. The trainer fixture mismatch is
unresolved; this model change does not suppress state validation.
