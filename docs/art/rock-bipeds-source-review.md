# Graveler and Golem source review

The [official Graveler](https://sg.portal-pokemon.com/pokedex/0075/) and
[official Golem](https://sg.portal-pokemon.com/pokedex/0076/) pages returned
200. Their named primary artworks were individually inspected in background
image-only tabs. No reference media is copied into the repository.

Graveler is one broad irregular grey boulder with its face on that body,
a jagged raised forehead field, narrow ivory gaze, pink grin, two larger
spread upper arms, two shorter lower arms, broad hands with actual blunt
digits and small block-like supporting feet. It does not have a separate
round head above a humanoid torso. Golem has a large nearly spherical shell
of fitted polygonal grey-green stones separated by dark brown seams, a small
reptilian tan-grey head with a broad brow/snout, narrow red gaze, open pink
mouth and ivory teeth, short clawed arms and stocky clawed feet. Its stones
form the actual shell rather than three rows of protruding brown balls.

The paired 1600×824 baseline software render exited zero and was fully
inspected. Both current models have separate oval toy heads, protruding round
eyes, generic polygon torsos, fists without fingers and flat oval feet.
Graveler's two arm pairs lack the different proportions and hand shapes.
Golem's shell is scattered brown polyhedra over a generic torso; its reptilian
head, teeth, clawed limbs and complete fitted shell are absent.

The [PoGo-Devs asset repository](https://github.com/PoGo-Devs/PoGo-3D-Assets)
advertises rigged species and names Models Resource and ROE Studios as its
sources. Its root contains no license file and the GitHub repository API
returns no detected license. Its progress table is not inspected rig/skin
data or permission to reuse the game models. No asset archive was downloaded
or imported from it.

No external mesh, rig, skin or animation was retrieved or installed in this
review. Animation listings are not verified accessible rig contents, and zero
listed clips does not establish that a model lacks a rig. These specific
checks do not prove that no suitable open model exists.
