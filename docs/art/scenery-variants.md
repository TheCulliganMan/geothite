# 3D scenery variants

The Rust mesh builders generate three related assets for each repeated scenery
family. Route pines use spreading, upright, and swept branch recipes. Park trees
use broad, tall, and open crowns. Cut shrubs use low rounded, upright, and swept
crowns. Coastal and boundary stones use broad, upright, and sloping shoulders;
cave stones select three fracture families while retaining their mineral and ice
finishes.

`scenery_variants::index` hashes the original source tile coordinates into three
choices. Multi-cell objects choose once at their source origin. Cropped render
regions add their source grid origin before selecting, so camera movement and
rebuilding a region retain the same asset. Small variations within the existing
foliage recipes remain deterministic as well.

`exterior_variants.rs` generates and caches the three derivatives of each original
closed exterior mesh. Rocks have inclined shoulders, weathered face normals,
and three subtle mineral palettes instead of smooth pale oval shading. The
derivatives retain triangle topology and material seams, transform normals
with the deformation's inverse transpose, stay inside
the original footprint, and keep their bottom on the original ground plane.
Pine and park assets are generated by their existing native Rust mesh builders.
No textures, content packs, collision, scripts, or 2D render paths change.
