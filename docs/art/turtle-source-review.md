# Wartortle and Blastoise source and baseline review

The [official Wartortle artwork](https://sg.portal-pokemon.com/pokedex/0008/)
and [normal Blastoise artwork](https://sg.portal-pokemon.com/pokedex/0009/)
were individually inspected in background image-only browser tabs. Wartortle's
pale blue feather ears and broad cloud tail, blue-violet skin, pointed gaze,
small fangs, shell plates and compact clawed limbs need individual treatment.
Blastoise needs a substantial domed plated shell, ivory rim, powerful limbs,
compact pointed ears, short blue tail and two actual hollow shoulder cannons.
The ordinary Blastoise reference was selected by its image's explicit alt text;
Mega and Gigantamax forms are separate and outside this replacement.

The [kishi Wartortle metadata](https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/f64ed3dc727a4c848e2e11a71d93a269)
advertises downloadable CC BY 4.0 geometry, 745,126 faces, zero animation clips
and an empty description. Its download endpoint returned HTTP 401. No model
or skeleton was retrieved or imported.

The [Eire Wartortle metadata](https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/befac6ff79234c2fb980d3380a1608ce)
advertises downloadable CC BY 4.0 geometry, 2,252 faces, zero animation clips
and an empty description. Its download endpoint also returned HTTP 401. No
model or skeleton was retrieved or imported. Zero clips do not establish that
either model lacks bones, and a downloadable label does not prove access or
validated authoring provenance.

The [nguyenlouis32 Blastoise metadata](https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/417667e24bf14139b4de351c9b79b789)
advertises downloadable CC BY 4.0 geometry, 6,406 faces and zero animation clips.
Its description supplies Pokémon lore, without establishing an authoring
source. Its download endpoint returned HTTP 401; no geometry or skeleton was
retrieved or imported.

The [anak3d Wartortle metadata](https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/300cb31ab60f422b8c7d6bd7b1f6e39d)
and [anak3d Blastoise metadata](https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/83ec8dfd0f954629825dcb4160d8d4ba)
were found through the creator's Pokémon collection API. Each reports one
animation and states that the author models, textures and animates in Blender.
Each explicitly reports `isDownloadable: false` and a null license. Neither
provides an accessible open rig for this repository, and neither was imported.
This is a review of specific candidates, not an exhaustive source search.


Both installed baseline meshes were rendered in a software oblique view with
exit zero and individually inspected. Both show the old protruding rose eye
spheres, simple rounded arms and feet, oversized shell rim and disconnected
horizontal belly seams. Wartortle has angular purple ears and a plain tubular
tail instead of the pale feather/cloud shapes. Blastoise's barrel ends are
filled by protruding dark rods rather than actual recessed openings. These
are concrete replacement defects; neither current model is visually approved.
