The authored region and its implementation evidence are in the
[Minnesota handoff](../../planning/minnesota/README.md) and
[current status](../../planning/minnesota/STATUS.md). Native review includes the
twenty routes, Grand Marais and the free Boundary Waters basecamp; an earned
Winona-to-Ely adventure remains unfinished.

The combined ignored `content-packs/minnesota-northstar-harbor.crystalpack`
contains 502 maps, retains the repaired three-city/prairie/geography source and adds the
[Team Lode story](../../planning/minnesota/STORY.md): a real mine foreman battle,
separate meter/test-log recovery, Grand Rapids handover and guarded R09/R10
walking links, the Duluth Kettlurk reveal, Grand Marais foreshadowing and the
chief/receiver finale.
Main story flags come from separate native interactions; defeating the foreman
does not grant the instrument or depot clearance. The technician keeps the
meter and gives a log copy for Duluth after the player acknowledges the reading.
The harbor technician names the intended captive and establishes that Lode
continued after receiving warnings. All nine pages precede the signal event;
the interaction keeps the existing eight-badge/depot checks and gives no items.

The [Grand Marais design and fast review loop](../../planning/minnesota/playable/README.md)
remain available as an isolated town slice. Every map uses authored source tiles;
earlier ImageGen plates and generic blockouts are not accepted final geography.
Other hubs, Duluth regional travel integration, upstream earned chapters, League
completion and the Kettlurk set piece still require implementation. Final user visual
approval remains open. Generated packs and renders must stay ignored.
