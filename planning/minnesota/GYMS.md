# Minnesota gym guide

The Field and Intro editions of Town Returns v01 have the same eight badge challenges. All 18 ordinary gym trainers can spot you when you cross their line of sight. To challenge a leader, face them and press A. Varel checks that you delivered Ivy’s parcel in Rochester. Each later leader checks the preceding badge.

| Gym | Leader | Type | Badge | Prize TM |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rochester | Varel | Normal | Care | Headbutt |
| Mankato | Torven | Ground | Prairie | Mud-Slap |
| Minneapolis | Avrin | Electric | Current | ThunderPunch |
| St. Paul | Brannic | Steel | Capitol | Steel Wing |
| St. Cloud | Saven | Flying | Wind | Swift |
| Brainerd | Sivren | Water | Lake | Rain Dance |
| Bemidji | Orlis | Grass | Pine | Giga Drain |
| Duluth | Norek | Ice | Harbor | Icy Wind |

If your Pack cannot take the TM, the badge is still yours. Make room and talk to that leader again to collect the prize. [Installed badge, TM and trainer checks](revisions/campaign-quality-v01/check-gyms.py)

## Rochester

Walk north from the entrance into Tavel’s sight. Beating him opens both ends of the court. The west stairs take you around to Varel. The shorter east stairs pass Elvik if you want a second fight. [Court and sightlines](gyms/floorplans-20261117/rochester-contract.json)

## Mankato

Beat Caldren at the foot of the west stairs, then climb and drop off the low ledge into the central quarry. Jorel watches the path below. His defeat opens the east stairs to Torven. To retreat from the quarry, take the lower ledge back toward the lobby. [Terraces, drops and stair gates](gyms/overhaul-20261117/mankato_notes.md)

## Minneapolis

Beat Lenvi on the west approach and switch on the feeder beside her. Cross its bridge into the middle gallery. Beat Jossa, then face north at her console and switch on the east feeder. Leave both on to reach Avrin. If you switch a bridge off while exploring, the one-way service drop on the east side leads back to the entrance. [Feeder positions and bridge states](gyms/overhaul-20261117/minneapolis_notes.md)

## St. Paul

Choose a lift from the lobby. The left lift leads to Belva in the archives. The right lift leads to Orven in the workshop. One victory opens Brannic’s court. Take the back lift in the wing you chose. The pads work in both directions if you want to return or visit the other wing. [Lift and court contract](gyms/floorplans-20261117/st-paul-contract.json)

## St. Cloud

Beat Wyrel on the lower west bank. Set the fan east and ride the lower lane across. From the far bank, set it west and take the upper lane back to Saven. There’s also a battle route. Beating Hallen on the far bank opens the central catwalk and qualifies you once Wyrel is beaten. [Relay and catwalk contract](gyms/continuation-20261117/st-cloud-contract.json)

## Brainerd

From between the two cedar posts, **walk straight north eight steps** through the pale shallows. You’ll be facing Sivren on her sandbar. Press A to challenge her with the Wind Badge. You can walk this whole course, including the return. Lorla watches the west shore and Bravel watches the east. Both fights are optional. [Beach layout and trainer positions](gyms/brainerd-lakeside-v01/README.md)

## Bemidji

The entrance guide offers HM Cut if you need it. Teach it from the TM/HM pocket. The south hedge leads to Moslen in the west clearing. The north hedge leads to Elvren. Cut the trees and beat both keepers in either order to reach Orlis. Walking through a cut opening keeps it clear on return. Brindel is optional. His defeat opens a trail between the clearings. [Hedges and connecting trail](gyms/continuation-20261117/bemidji-contract.json)

## Duluth

Take the west ice course to Livra. Her defeat opens the east launch. Aim for the rough patch at the top of the east course to reach Tordel. **Livra and Tordel are the two required wins.** Anver watches a side stop. To reach Norek from Tordel’s course, touch the east wall, slide south to rough ice, turn west, then go north. After winning the Harbor Badge, use the newly opened west return to skate back to the city doors. The harbor crew’s signal is a separate story step. [Ice routes, sightlines and victory exit](revisions/return-visits-v01/check-harbor-gym.py)
