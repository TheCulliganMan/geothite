# Minnesota playtest task list

User-reported issues from live play on November 1, 2026. All tasks are open.
Keep these numbers stable when adding reports or recording fixes.

- [x] **1. Rewrite unnatural, repetitive dialogue.** Reduce exposition across the cast. Give characters distinct, concise lines with a purpose. Remove duplicated NPC/sign messages, including repeated “we keep records” language.
- [x] **2. Clarify Tamarack's story and the current objective.** Explain what is happening, why the player is involved, and what to do next. Update NPC guidance as the player completes story milestones.
- [x] **3. Restore Tamarack's expected overworld sprites.** The authored sprites appear to be missing from the deployed game. Check the deployed content and sprite bindings against the intended artwork.
- [x] **4. Fix unexplained guard disappearance.** Guards disappear after the player receives a Pokémon. Give that progression change a clear story explanation and coherent behavior.
- [x] **5. Repair the adventure-tracking child NPC.** The child who supposedly tracks the player's adventure does not appear to work. Check the interaction and its response to actual progress.
- [ ] **6. Fix incorrect Cut prompts.** Pressing A in grass produces “this tree can be cut.” Cut-related text also appears after battles without a relevant tree interaction. Resolve the actual interaction target and prevent stale or unrelated prompts.
- [ ] **7. Fix trainer-to-battle music transitions.** Trainer encounter music begins but is not replaced by battle music when the battle starts.
- [ ] **8. Correct ordinary trainer behavior.** Increase sight distance, make trainers turn more frequently, and have ordinary trainers initiate battles without asking the player whether to battle.
- [ ] **9. Contain battle presentation.** Rain effects and overworld sprites remain visible outside the main battle screen. Hide overworld presentation while the battle interface owns the screen.
- [ ] **10. Correct Mart arrival positions.** Entering a Mart places the player several tiles too far inside. Align each affected entrance warp with its intended arrival tile.
- [ ] **11. Stop region-transition and prompt flicker.** Region transitions and their accompanying prompts visibly flicker.
- [ ] **12. Remove HM moves from generated wild Pokémon.** Wild Pokémon should not arrive with HM moves that leave the player stuck with an unwanted move. Review wild moveset generation and affected regional encounters.
- [ ] **13. Review the early Winona–Rochester cave.** The route appears to force the player through a confusing, poorly laid-out cave that seems to require Flash too early. Confirm whether this cave belongs on the route, remove unintended progression blockers, and make the intended passage understandable and traversable.
- [ ] **14. Repair the Pokémon switching menu.** Party rows overlap, HP information is missing for some Pokémon, and the bottom “Which Pokémon?” prompt contains garbled text. Use the reported mobile screenshot as the visual reference: [party switching menu](renders/user-reports/2026-11-01/party-switch-menu.jpg).
- [x] **15. Address the missing Pokégear handoff.** The player reports never receiving a Pokégear and has no clear indication of when it is supposed to be awarded. Identify the intended story milestone, make the handoff reliable, and explain it clearly to the player.
- [x] **16. Relocate and rewrite the fossil hunter.** The fossil hunter standing in the middle of grass feels misplaced. Move the character onto the cliff and give the character dialogue that makes sense for that location and activity.
- [x] **17. Restore Mom's intended door approach.** Mom never comes to the door. Review the scene against the normal Pokémon Crystal source behavior and give the encounter coherent actor movement and positioning.
- [x] **18. Fix the rival's approach before conversation.** The rival does not come up close to speak. Complete the intended approach and stop at the appropriate adjacent tile before starting the conversation.
- [x] **19. Fix approach-to-dialogue sequencing.** Characters begin speaking before reaching the tile next to the player. Finish their authored movement and facing before opening dialogue, following normal Crystal scene behavior.
- [x] **20. Use normal Crystal scenes as the writing and staging reference.** Study how the original source sequences encounters, movement, facing, and short, purposeful dialogue. Apply that standard to the Minnesota story and store interactions; replace rambling exposition with conversations that fit the scene and clearly communicate their purpose.
- [ ] **21. Make connecting caves geographically coherent.** Apply this rule to every cave connecting surface locations: its underground layout must span those locations in a direction consistent with their relative surface positions, like a basement beneath the area. Entrances and exits must correspond to the places above them. A vertical descent cannot substitute for traveling between different places. In particular, a Winona–Rochester cave must provide an actual underground passage from Winona toward Rochester, rather than going straight down and emerging somewhere unrelated.
- [ ] **22. Prevent mandatory darkness before Flash is available.** Never require the player to navigate an unlit cave before the normal progression provides an opportunity to obtain and use Flash, including any required permissions. Required early caves must be lit or have a usable alternative route. Review the Winona–Rochester passage and every other mandatory cave against the actual order of progression.
- [x] **23. Verify Reed and other updated characters use their new sprites.** Reed and other characters may still be showing older battle or overworld sprites. Check both presentations against the intended authored assets and confirm the deployed pack includes and selects the correct versions. Record which characters are affected; the report is currently a suspected mismatch.
