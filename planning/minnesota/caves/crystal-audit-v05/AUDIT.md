# Crystal cave comparison

## What falls short across the region

- **The maps keep the same basic silhouette.** In the v04 floor plans, several rooms are a broad dry belt around one pool or quiet rock field. The amount and placement of solid rock seldom distinguish an entrance chamber from the next decision. Rock Tunnel puts thick rock masses where they hide one ladder while revealing another. Mt. Mortar varies the shape and scale of the entry, the upper expedition and the wet basement. [Rock Tunnel](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/Pokemon-CrystalVersion-Kanto-RockTunnel.png) · [Mt. Mortar](https://vgmaps.de/maps/view?m=20189)
- **The cameras show props before they explain routes.** The previous pictures often center an item or a pair of stairs while the bank, cliff or corner that directs movement is offscreen. Repeated horizontal cliff strips and isolated rocks give unrelated caves a familiar texture without a recognizable landmark. Slowpoke Well uses the cut between landings and water to explain exactly what can be reached from each shelf. [Slowpoke Well](https://vgmaps.de/maps/view?m=20161)
- **Walking room is often unused.** Several broad walks admit four or more parallel ways past a landmark and have the same view for their whole length. Union Cave leaves a broad bank where a battle, a water approach or a stair needs room and narrows the connecting pieces. Some rooms in Crystal are deliberately spacious, including the stopping board on Ice Path B2F. Their width serves an action. [Union Cave](https://vgmaps.de/maps/view?m=20159) · [Ice Path](https://vgmaps.de/maps/view?m=20196)
- **Side trips offer too little change in pace.** A reward often sits against the far wall of the main room. Original Crystal uses changes in level, narrow mouths and different travel abilities to turn rewards into destinations. The floors that return a player to a familiar landmark often give a shorter or different way home. [Mt. Mortar](https://vgmaps.de/maps/view?m=20189)
- **Some cliffs lose their edge at player scale.** In daylight examples from v04, walkable gold and the stippled field look busy at the same time. In other rooms, a few passable ledge quadrants carry exactly the texture of the solid background. The important distinction is the face between the levels. Crystal's stipple reads as quiet space because a short profile separates it from the route. [Union Cave](https://vgmaps.de/maps/view?m=20159)
