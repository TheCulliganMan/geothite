# Voyageurs Ice

The original Ice Path opens with an ordinary walking approach, then has a
large slide board and a second, much smaller puzzle for a separate reward.
Its wide surface is a specific stopping puzzle. The return through the east
wing and the lower boards ask the player to orient differently. Rock, land
and ice each explain a different action on one Game Boy screen.
[Original Ice Path floors](https://vgmaps.de/maps/view?m=20196)

| Floor | What the v04 pictures and routes show | What the new plan needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Voyageurs Ice 1F | The 20×10 outline is almost entirely a horizontal strip. The entry opens straight onto a broad east slide. The Ice Heal sits in the middle of the route and the attached stair reads like a prop beside it. The second sheet extends the same view with scattered rocks. The far ladder is on a pale slab along the outer edge. The Full Heal spur has very little visual identity. | Give the entrance a short dry approach and a distinct view of the first launch. Change the direction of the frozen creek at a dry turning bank. Separate the far ladder shelf from the optional lower trough so each has its own approach and camera. |
| Voyageurs Ice B1F | The 12×17 outline repeats the upper floor with the long axis turned vertically. The Revive and NeverMeltIce again sit beside stairs or circular rocks in pale open ground. The thin upright vein splits the art, yet the slide still feels like another corridor. A player returning after the Revive sees the same broad stripe again. | Let the first chute make a deliberate turn into a protected Revive nook. Give the deep reward a separate wide stopping pool around a solid headland. A distinct lower return should lead back to the familiar dry bank. Keep the route safe in every pickup state. |

The first floor should teach with a short series of distinct stops. The
basement should then use that movement to make a side trip and a deeper
choice. The guide, surveyor, existing rewards and paired ladder records
give the two floors continuity.

## Replacement

The 1F entrance now has a dry shelf from which the first launch is visible.
The player reaches the Ice Heal on the turning bank before dropping to the
second sheet. That sheet runs across the bottom of the room and bends back
up to the ladder. The hidden Full Heal has its own low pocket. The shortest
walk and slide route from the outside landing to the ladder uses 24 inputs,
including five slides. The return uses different stops.

The B1F survey landing opens onto two different choices. A short slide reaches
the Revive stair and shelf. A vertical chute and a wide shelf below the
headland lead to the NeverMeltIce and the separate PP Up pocket. Both routes
return to the upper landing when either visible item has been collected.
Quieter blue ice marks solid fill and the camera border, and connected faces
show where the dry shelves step down.
