# Southern caves against Pokémon Crystal

## What fails in v04

I inspected the original Game Boy Color floor plates at full size and compared them with the six whole v04 rooms and their native 10 × 9 walking-tile cameras. Distances below count legal steps from the compiled arrival to a usable adjacent square with residents and pickups present. These are map measurements.

- **Driftless Hollow.** The west guide, Antidote shelf and rookie sit in wide lobes joined by equally wide floor. The first lower elbow looks like plain brown floor threaded through wallpaper. The trainer-free passage and main passage converge around the same middle space, and the 58-step through trip gives little reason to walk 34 steps from the west entrance to the Gallery stair. The original Union Cave 1F repeatedly changes the through route by turning it around actual ponds and long ridges, and gives its stairs a recognizable platform. Dark Cave uses one tight, unmistakable passage into a broad later section. Hollow needs an active route fork, a distinct shelf for the Gallery, and a spur with its own visible destination. [Union Cave plate](https://vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/Pokemon-CrystalVersion-Johto-UnionCave.png) · [Dark Cave plate](https://vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/Pokemon-CrystalVersion-Johto-DarkCave.png)
- **Lamp Gallery.** The first turn and lower floor are a long winding ribbon. In the lit views, visually interchangeable rectangles, stairs and boulder edges compete with the relief. Before the player reaches it, the ladder is 44 steps behind them. The cache costs at least another circuit through the upper shelf and is 53 steps from the arrival. Mt. Mortar 1F puts its small rewards in terraces reached by their own stairs, with the dry passage continually reframed by attached rock. The Blackthorn half of Dark Cave has a dramatically shorter gap between a constrained approach and its recognizable reward alcoves. The relief should be a chamber you can recognize and reach promptly, with a separate upper exploration choice and a consequential way back. [Mt. Mortar plate](https://vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/Pokemon-CrystalVersion-Johto-MtMortar.png) · [Dark Cave plate](https://vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/Pokemon-CrystalVersion-Johto-DarkCave.png)
- **River Pocket.** The front lake is an almost straight stripe beside a parallel dry walking strip; the lone island reward uses Surf, but most of the shoreline reads the same. The Strength pinch is 22 steps away and opens into a broad nondescript floor. The separate end pool is a slot against a wall, so the pillar and Castorock bed feel like events pinned to empty wings. Slowpoke Well B2F lets the water fill the chamber and gives every landing a distinct purpose. Union Cave B2F visibly alternates lake crossings, ledges, and reached platforms. River Pocket needs a recognizable water circuit, a clear approach to the boulder, and two visibly different post-Strength destinations. [Slowpoke Well plate](https://vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/Pokemon-CrystalVersion-Johto-SlowpokeWell.png) · [Union Cave plate](https://vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/Pokemon-CrystalVersion-Johto-UnionCave.png)
- **Rock Cut.** It takes 32 steps northbound from the south arrival. The healer sits only two steps in, yet the floor behind them remains a broad diagonal room with isolated stone patterns and drawn stairs that barely shape the trip. The visible Repel is only 26 steps from the south, much of it shared with the crossing. Diglett’s Cave is readable as a folded single route with tiny mouth lobbies, firm continuous flanks, and one unusual cache rock. The useful idea is an edited passage profile and a short detour with a price, rather than copying Diglett’s diagonal or its three areas. [Diglett’s Cave plate](https://vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/Pokemon-CrystalVersion-Kanto-Diglett%27sCave.png)
- **Cedar Shelter.** The entry camera mostly looks along a plain bank and the healer chamber is approximately 33 dry steps from it. The Ether is 20 steps away, already visible from the broad east bank before its little recess makes a choice apparent. Surf shortens these trips to 23 and 16 steps, respectively, but the lake's edges are so uniformly open that the shorter wet lines lack deliberate landings. Slowpoke Well stages each raised shelf around a staged entry to water. Mt. Mortar’s entry floor makes the basins force different paths to different stairs. Cedar needs a visible service-turnout near the entry, a stronger relationship between the spring and the deeper cache, and practical wet and dry approaches. [Slowpoke Well plate](https://vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/Pokemon-CrystalVersion-Johto-SlowpokeWell.png) · [Mt. Mortar plate](https://vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/Pokemon-CrystalVersion-Johto-MtMortar.png)
- **Granite Den.** The 39-step crown trip and 29-step lichen trip climb the same large annulus for much of their length. The seep is pushed into the bottom-right corner and contributes little to the upper route. Multiple long, straight shelf lines read as comparable climbable routes, while the almost identical floor on either side makes the actual return ledge hard to pick out. Dark Cave’s Blackthorn side alternates rock tongues, stair landings, and narrowing shoreline to make each rise a separate decision. Granite needs a clear crag-over-seep composition, a rewarding upper spur, and a shorter downhill loop whose departure and landing make sense in the same camera. [Dark Cave plate](https://vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/Pokemon-CrystalVersion-Johto-DarkCave.png)

## Layout changes

- **Driftless Hollow** now splits after the guide. A lower route folds around a rock spine and rejoins near the east exit. The high route links the Antidote and Gallery shelf to the rookie's sightline. The west-to-east crossing falls from 58 to 52 steps and the Gallery approaches from the west in 26 steps.
- **Lamp Gallery** loses five unused metatile columns. One narrow rise leads to the illuminated wall, and the cache belongs to the route behind that wall. The relief returns to the ladder in 19 steps across the ledge or 28 by the ordinary ramp. The paired warp indices and Flash check carry across the entrance and relief moves.
- **River Pocket** puts the west pool against two distinct shore shelves and puts a broken water bowl beneath the far pillar. The Strength boulder remains the only way into the eastern chambers, including for a player with Surf. The island cache remains a separate water trip from the near bank.
- **Rock Cut** joins two distinct mouth chambers with a narrow bent passage. The upper pocket has the visible Repel and the middle bend hides the Hard Stone. From the north lip the drop returns to the south in 31 steps. The ordinary route takes 38.
- **Cedar Shelter** places Harper in a seven-step turnout off the arrival. The spring fills the center and breaks the east shore, so reaching the Ether takes a western stair and 34 steps by land or a 28-step Surf route across the bowl.
- **Granite Den** puts its seep under the main cliff and separates the reader's bend from the two upper finds. A full return from the hidden crown shelf is 27 steps across the western ledge or 40 by the dry climb.

Comparing both native palettes on the rebuilt floor plans led to fixed night lighting for Hollow and Cedar. The quieter blue-purple rock takes the glare out of the spring. Traversable edges at the River Pocket boulder, the Rock Cut stair, and the outer faces now carry the continuous dark walking surface. Their cliff pixels and collision stay aligned.

`check_south.py` compares the compiled cave layouts with the authored rows. It also checks the seven real exterior mouths, passive landings, item and NPC approaches, water boundaries, directed ledge routes, the Flash condition, and all three River Pocket field-move states.
