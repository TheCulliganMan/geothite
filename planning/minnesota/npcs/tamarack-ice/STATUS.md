# Tamarack, Team ICE and eight trainer types

This work extends the selected current regional pack, resolved through
`world.json` before publication. Existing story characters keep their scripts,
teams, rewards and progression. Native art uses six 16×16 field poses and a
matched 56×56 battle reference with four RGB555 inks. Generated banks and packs
remain ignored.
