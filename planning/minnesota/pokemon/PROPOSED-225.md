## Design briefs

- **P01 — Snugslug → Mantlesnail → Cloisterra:** A small sheltering shell develops protected chambers and a broad enclosing lip. Preserve obvious snail anatomy; do not repeat agate crystals or fortress shields.
- **P02 — Flurrit → Hareskift:** Broad sweeping paws erase its trail; layered fur develops into a shifting drift-shaped outline. Keep the two-stage arrangement provisional.
- **P03 — Owletch → Hollowl → Gloamowl:** Broken plumage patterns become openings in an owl silhouette; the final stage resembles a bird-shaped absence in northern woods.
- **P04 — Bitterry:** A small woody berry-cluster creature displays bright decoy berries around a concealed stern face. Fictional poisonous lure powers; a living plant, with no dough, baking or food-container anatomy.
- **P05 — Cubluff → Talursa:** A black cub with blunt basalt claws becomes a broad four-legged bear whose interlocking shoulder columns and fallen-rock back plates embody a moving shoreline cliff.
- **P06 — Trailpup → Pathfang → Waywolf:** Small forked paw markings develop into interlocking trail bands around legs and shoulders. The adult leaves temporary luminous paths its pack can follow. Invented path magic; avoid copper anatomy, aurora curtains and borrowed cultural teachings.
- **P07 — Hushbat → Deepulse → Noctector:** Large ears and layered wing membranes become enclosed sensing chambers. A quiet folded bat develops a broad detector-like silhouette that senses rare invisible crossings. Fictional psychic detection inspired by the underground lab; avoid mining lamps, ore loads and musical echo silhouettes.
- **P08 — Burrowbill → Bankwing:** A small earth-dusted bird develops broad excavating claws, a reinforced beak and curved wing-root channels resembling tunnel arches. Aerial burrow construction rather than a carried nest or building.
- **P09 — Snowpip → Squallfinch → Whitewhirl:** Small dark-and-white wing marks become broad curving snow-streamer feather vanes. The adult gathers windblown snow into a moving spiral around its flock. Keep this airborne weather silhouette distinct from the hare and stationary snow-fence line.
- **P10 — Skimnymph → Rimefoil:** A low long-bodied youngster grows six blade-edged ice-running legs; the adult unfolds four taut sail-like wings and steers over ice using its own abdomen as a boom. No separate boat, hockey equipment or fishing flag. Both stages share Ice/Dragon; maintain recognizable insect-derived anatomy.
