[The verified rules and build](../../../docs/northwoods-fairy.md) describe the local v2 pack and completed battle/animation checks.

The current curated roster has **223 active species across 101 families: 40 three-stage families, 42 two-stage families and 19 single-stage species**. It retains six legendaries, two mythicals and the three-stage mosquito pseudo-legendary at the end. The source catalog below also retains retired families and proposals; source IDs are stable and differ from the densely repacked active Pokédex numbers. `roster-curation.json` determines what is active.

LINE087 is restored in current authoring as pure-Psychic Jotlet → Memofold → Recallisk, a one → three → seven sticky-note keeper. Stable source IDs 186–188 and app LINE087 remain; no other family is cut. Its six native views, final 474-species/270-move pack and candidate 13 web integration are verified; all three production tests pass and release 13 is published, with 2,038 exact public-byte plus six status checks passing. Published release12 retains the prior 220-species snapshot.

The complete **313-species source** has 52 three-stage families, 66 two-stage families and 25 single-stage species. Earlier checkpoints and every render remain archived. Runtime stats, learnsets and native art are separately authored and validated.

New ordinary solo **Kensigard (MN_277 / LINE126)** is pure Ground, with 475 total stats (90/105/110/35/40/95 in HP/Attack/Defense/Speed/Special Attack/Special Defense order), genderless Mineral biology and no evolution. Its Kensington/Viking-inspired carved tablet opens lake country before Riceling. [Runestone Museum](https://runestonemuseum.org/runestone/) and [MNopedia](https://www.mnhs.org/mnopedia/search/index/thing/kensington-runestone) ground the physical slab and local authenticity debate; the creature and its powers are fiction. Release 09 is publicly verified with all 1,926 exact-byte checks; 08 remains retained history.

The final Taconarch redraw is approved: a low broad quadrupedal ore wyrm with layered magnetic armor, pellet shoulder/hip joints, dark rail ribs and a curled tail. Its first two stages, types, individual palettes and balanced profile remain unchanged. The frozen v5 Taconarch pack passed its four production tests. The eleven newly integrated families have passed fresh merged v6 production validation and final local web integration. Release 09 is published and publicly verified; release 08 remains retained history.

The current mitten line is Mitwist → Mispair → Shawlter, Ghost/Fairy → Ghost/Fairy → Fairy/Flying. Both Chislet and Buttermuse are Ice/Fairy, with Buttermuse's carved-butter princess identity. Their earlier seiche and fighting-butter designs remain archived.

MN_101–MN_150 add forty prairie and ten Iron Range species; MN_151–MN_153 form the giant-mosquito pseudo-legendary line. The original 153 species retain their identities in the expansion.
