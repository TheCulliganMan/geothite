# Birchit and Voyagear native refinement

This package contains only the baby and adult beavers. Root owns the approved Portabeau middle stage and combines the final six-view family. No canonical manifest, roster, profile or palette values were changed here.

Birchit keeps its small golden body, brown ears/limbs, white forelock and accepted upright birch-paddle tail. Voyagear keeps its rust tuque with navy band, white arrow waist sash, one straight canoe paddle across its shoulders, hand-on-hip pose and small natural navy tail attached low behind the hip. The paddle is distinct from the tail. Its shaft uses white instead of introducing a fifth wood color.

Both were redrawn through built-in ImageGen from the accepted v9 identities and the coarse middle-stage style reference. The first attempts preserved too much white muzzle material. Focused v2 edits removed the white cheek/muzzle patches, separated the eyes/forelock/teeth, and enlarged clear pupil and tooth-separator ink. Every prompt and all four generated source images are retained. Rear views remain full body at root's explicit request for consistency with the current family source.

The prompt requests true 36/54-pixel cluster design and exact four-color RGB555 palettes. The returned sources still contain slight intermediate color variation and some fine seams; the selected native conversion quantizes to exactly the declared four colors and preserves facial ink. The final native frames, rather than the enlarged source alone, determine acceptance.
