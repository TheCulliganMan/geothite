# Beaver starter refinement

Birchit → Portabeau → Voyagear is the selected Water starter. Names, elemental
progression and individual RGB555 palettes remain stable.

- Birchit: accepted seated kit and attached upright birch-pattern tail.
- Portabeau: lean adolescent canoe porter. Bare head, natural ears and white
  forelock; no hat or paddle. Compact canvas canoe pack, broad flap and straps,
  arrow sash, small natural tail attached below the waist.
- Voyagear: mature French-Canadian voyageur. Rust tuque, navy coat-shaped fur,
  arrow sash and small natural tail attached at the lower back. Carries one
  straight wooden canoe paddle, with aligned shaft and blade, at the shoulder.
  The user withdrew the bent-paddle variation.

The canoe pack, sash and separate natural tail must be readable at native game
size. Large white muzzle shapes must not merge the eyes and teeth. Black pupils,
short buck teeth and their separation need deliberate pixel clusters.

## Retained iterations

All built-in ImageGen sources and exact prompts are saved here. v4 improved the
voyageur clothing but still carried logs. v5 substituted paddles. v6 tried a
straight/bent progression; v7 removed the middle hat and paddle, adding the canoe
pack. v8 straightened the adult paddle. v9 attempted a whole-family native-pixel
redraw. v10 isolates the middle views with coarser clusters; v11 reforms its
muzzle, eyes and short teeth. Earlier conversions remain candidates, not approvals.
The photo references only document the withdrawn real bent-shaft proposal.

Native candidates and comparisons are retained under
`target/minnesota-pokemon/proposals/MN_F004-*` and copied to the immutable creature
