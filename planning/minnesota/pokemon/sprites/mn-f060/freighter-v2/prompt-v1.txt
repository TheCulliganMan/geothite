Subject: OREVIATHAN, the Great Lakes ore-freighter creature. Fire/Water type. This is a living Pokemon whose own anatomy embodies bulk lake shipping and steam pressure. NOT a ship sitting on an animal, NOT a toy model with a face pasted on, NOT a human in ship armor. No carried tools, no ropes or independent accessories, no buildings on its back.

Evolved silhouette: huge shoulders and a broad continuous lake-blue hull chest narrow into a low pointed bow-like belly. Two small heavy flipper feet brace its bottom in a wide stance; a short ridged stern tail balances behind. The near arm is a massive triangular GANTRY formed by the creature's upper arm and sharply bent elbow, with a thick steam piston as the other side of its triangular anatomy; it ends in a wide ore-grab forehand with only THREE heavy blunt scoop fingers. The other arm is lifted outward as a smaller foreshortened gantry and scoop hand. Both arm openings are real clear negative-space holes, readable at 56 pixels. Face and family connection: keep Lakernaut's sturdy compact face at the front-center of the hull chest, with two bold white eyes under a broad black brow and a short copper squared muzzle. Twin thick curved smokestack horns grow behind its head, each ending in a simple separate white steam curl with black contour. Their root shape clearly grows from Lakernaut's two stack horns. Steam curls are small and connected to their stack rim, not scattered particles. This final has a much wider body, heavier triangular crane arms and lower center of gravity than Lakernaut, visibly the largest of a three-stage family. Fierce but appealing intelligent face, memorable Gen2 monster shape.
