# Superior mirage — revision 4 proposal

Loomlet and Inverveil keep their identities and individual palettes. The elevated pale false face is now visibly separated from the small dark real body. The true white eyes remain below the illusion; the upper face grows from a narrow teardrop to a wide inverted shield with repeated horizontal refraction bands.

This deliberately replaces the previous solid fan and bent neck. The illusion is fixed opaque pixel art with a white gap and a pale stepped streak, so it needs no transparency, gradient or new renderer feature. The profile amendments describe that visual change only; stats, types, level-32 evolution, moves and rarity are preserved.

Built-in ImageGen produced `front-back.imagegen-v4.png` from the exact `prompt-v4.txt`. The original tool output and every converted variant remain saved. All files here are proposals; the canonical family manifest and previous sources are untouched.

The selected manifest uses SourceBacked contours, white-detail threshold 4 and interior-black threshold 12. Solid was rejected because it turned the pale refraction streak into a black structural neck. White threshold 2 did not improve the native result.
