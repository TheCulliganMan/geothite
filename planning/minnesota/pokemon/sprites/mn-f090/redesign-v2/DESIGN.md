# LINE090 cookie-pail redesign proposal

Doughlin (MN195) is preserved exactly. Crumband (MN196) and Jarbaron (MN197) retain their names, Fire/Dark typing, stats, evolution thresholds and moves. Physical growth connects the stages: a curled cookie ear becomes a C-shaped structural handle, then a complete arch; a belly pocket becomes an open, overflowing cookie-pail body.

Crumband is broad and forward-leaning, with a shallow warm hoard cup and one large cookie peeking over its rim. The face is a chocolate-chip plane in the rim, and two crescent-cookie arms clamp inward. Jarbaron grows a deep tapered pail torso, high arch handle, three overflowing cookies, thick clamping arms and splayed baked feet. There is no separate animal head, costume, carried bucket, lid hat or spiky crown.

Each evolved species uses its own four-ink GBC palette, specified directly in prompt-v2.txt and palettes.json. The built-in ImageGen source is retained in front-back.imagegen-v2.png. Native candidates remain ignored tooling output. Doughlin is excluded from the conversion manifest so its authored pixels cannot drift.

This is a proposal only. Do not register, allocate IDs, modify roster files or replace active LINE090 assets before root reviews it. Integration should merge the four replacement view records with the two untouched Doughlin records and preserve its existing native outputs byte-for-byte.
