# LINE090: cookie → cone → bucket

Doughlin (MN195) remains exactly as authored. Proposed names: Crumcone (MN196, formerly Crumband) and Pailbaron (MN197, formerly Jarbaron). Both names are unique in the current roster and remain within ten ASCII letters. Existing Fire/Dark typing, stats, moves and evolution thresholds stay intact.

The progression follows Minnesota fair cookie servings: a cookie becomes a narrow white paper cookie cone, then a broad white cookie bucket. Faces sit on the white containers. Golden cookie heaps overflow each rim, with one curled cookie carrying Doughlin’s original curl forward. The cone has a long V silhouette and diagonal folded-paper seam. The pail has a rolled rim, high white bail handle, heavy cookie clamps and broad feet.

Each species has its own four-ink GBC palette: white, cookie gold, dark chocolate and black. Container bodies stay white. The large cookies retain warm gold and sparse chocolate chips. Exact RGB5, RGB555 and RGB8 definitions are in palettes.json and the generation prompt.

The primary reference is Sweet Martha’s official cone/pail serving description and white packaging photography. All creature anatomy and artwork are original; brand names, logos and printed artwork are excluded.

All v2 source and parameter variants are retained. This v3 directory is a proposal only. The conversion manifest intentionally excludes Doughlin, whose existing PNG, JSON and 2bpp hashes are recorded and preserved. No new IDs or canonical roster changes are made here.
