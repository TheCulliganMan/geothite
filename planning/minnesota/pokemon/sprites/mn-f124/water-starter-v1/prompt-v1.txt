Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: original Game Boy Color monster battle sprite sheet, THREE successive water starter evolutions, front and back. Design new iconic creatures in authentic Pokemon Crystal 2001 sprite language; do not reproduce an existing character.

SHEET: white background, THREE equal columns and TWO equal rows, generous empty white gutters. Column1 Rillip baby, column2 Sluimand middle, column3 Artesurge final. TOP ROW full body three-quarter FRONT views facing LEFT. BOTTOM ROW enlarged CLOSE REAR battle portraits facing upper RIGHT, nape and shoulders fill each cell, lower body deliberately cropped by bottom edge. Rear portraits are NOT smaller whole-body turnarounds. No lettering, labels, numbers, borders, color swatches, scenery, cast shadows or extra poses.

CORE IDEA: Minnesota spring thaw and underground artesian pressure become creature ANATOMY. Original low quadrupedal water guardians loosely inspired by a mudpuppy's friendliness, but invented bodies with integrated hollow water channels, not an ordinary salamander with stickers. One developmental motif evolves from a small open cheek duct into large hollow shoulder pressure vents, and from a broad curled tail into a thick bifurcated spillway tail. Water-filled body channels have broad white inner surfaces. No external stream effects: the sprite itself carries the concept. Strong actual growth: tiny round baby, athletic awkward adolescent, heavy protective adult with greatly expanded forequarters and powerful broad tail. Neither bipedal nor humanoid. NO external axolotl feather-gills, fin crown, trident, armor, rock plates, clothing, held objects or generic dragon anatomy. Do not resemble Wooper, Mudkip, Marshtomp, Swampert, Totodile or an otter.

RILLIP: cute tiny low pear-shaped reservoir body, oversized broad rounded head, short blunt muzzle and confident little smile, two large alert white eyes. Four short wide digging paws. Each cheek has ONE short thick upward-curled hollow duct shaped like a water scoop, fused into the cheek, not feathery. A single thick tail curves forward like a little open trough, with one white central channel. Big simple head/body masses; appealing friendly starter energy, asymmetric playful stance. Visibly smallest evolution.

SLUIMAND: longer sturdy torso, bigger splayed forepaws, smaller hindlegs, adolescent expression with one determined brow. The cheek ducts extend backward and fuse into TWO broad raised shoulder arches with clearly visible open dark holes, not ears or horns. A white channel continues from shoulder to thick flattened notched tail. Earthen-brown paws and lower trunk contrast with teal upper body. Strong side silhouette, unmistakable developmental bridge from small cheek scoop to huge shoulder pressure chamber.

ARTESURGE: imposing but friendly squat quadrupedal aquifer guardian, massive high forequarters, broad wedge-shaped head with a proud heavy brow and short muzzle, sturdy thick paws. TWO large hollow swept-back pressure arches are continuous parts of its shoulders, clearly open negative space through each arch. White inner channels wrap the shoulder and flank in one broad unbroken path. Thick muscular tail divides only at its broad upturned end into a stepped two-lipped spillway. Teal body, ochre earthen feet and belly. It looks capable of digging a spring and bracing against a torrent. Adult face remains related to Rillip; no giant fangs, no horns, no dragon wings, no ornamental clutter. Tail must be visibly anatomical, not a separate tool, and fit comfortably inside its cell.
