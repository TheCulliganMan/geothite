Two thick low earthen feet spread from the bottom like buried roots becoming square boots, with a shallow central gap. It stomps into soil to hold ground while its rune grooves slowly rearrange. Compact commanding stance, one foot forward and one arm slightly raised. No floating pieces or spiritual smoke. The sandstone and packed-earth anatomy are warm and weathered, never shiny metal.
