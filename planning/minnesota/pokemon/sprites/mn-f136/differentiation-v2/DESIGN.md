# Dozebear and Denumbra: distinct evolutionary stages

The previous two stages repeated a round seated bear, crescent arms and a circular chest-face. Their silhouettes now communicate different stages of a living dream den.

| Feature | Dozebear | Denumbra |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stance | Low, broad quadruped with folded forearms | Tall upright guardian on massive hind paws |
| Outline | Smooth horizontal back, soft haunches | High stepped shoulder mantle, deep vertical torso |
| Expression | Heavy eyelid and sleepy gaze | Alert outer bear eye, calm embedded dream-face |
| Chest den | Mostly closed narrow slit of dream-light | Tall open doorway framed by long crescent arms |
| Rear | Smooth low shoulder slope | Broad high mantle with prominent fur points |

The original per-species palettes, names, typing, stats and evolution are retained. Densy’s ten native PNG/index/2bpp/crop/review files remain byte-identical after export. The original front/back source and previous manifests remain saved.

The **built-in imagegen tool** produced the [new two-stage source](front-back.imagegen-v1.png) from the [exact redesign prompt](prompt-v1.txt). Native output uses the [accepted manifest](manifest-accepted.json): area-vote, solid exterior, Dozebear front fit48×44, Denumbra front48×54, both rear46×47. Nine guarded front pixels clarify Dozebear’s heavy eyelid; thirteen clarify Denumbra’s outer pupil and separate dream-face eyes. Source-center alternatives are preserved but rejected for scattered color pixels and broken contours.
