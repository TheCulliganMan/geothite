# Mudmender sprite redesign

Mudmender’s former gill panels and encircling tail hid the salamander inside. The new adult has a much broader exposed head, a gentle smile, shorter three-branch gill fans, visible neck/shoulders and planted feet. Its paddle tail curls beside the hindquarters as a partial cradle, leaving the face and forefeet readable. The close rear matches those head proportions and three gill pairs, facing upper right.

The original blue/plum palette, Water/Psychic identity and protective nursery theme are retained. Gillpup’s ten native PNG/index/2bpp/crop/review files remain byte-identical after the family export. The new sources apply only to Mudmender.

Created with the **built-in imagegen tool**. [Prompt1](prompt-v1.txt) redesigns the mature salamander; [prompt2](prompt-v2.txt) corrects the extra gill branch in the first candidate. Both generated source sheets are saved here as `front-back.imagegen-v1.png` and `front-back.imagegen-v2.png`. The first sheet is rejected for four near-side gill branches; the second has exactly three on each side in both views. All earlier original family artwork remains saved.

Selected [native recipe](manifest-accepted.json): area-vote sampling with solid exterior, original adult RGB555 `[32767,30418,17583,0]`, front fit54×52 and rear46×47. Four guarded front pixels clarify an eye catchlight and toe notches. Source-center output was rejected for broken facial rims and scattered color pixels.
