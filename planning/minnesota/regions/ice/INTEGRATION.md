## Geography

- **Rainy River Icefields:** a complete32×24 outdoor region west of the actual
  R20 border trail, with frozen backwaters, snowy pine banks, icy grass, useful
  slides/ordinary returns, optional trainers and a real warming hut. Its eastern
  join uses R20C00R00 west core `(0,44)`/`(0,45)`, reciprocal offset0. No fabricated
  International Falls town or R17 seam spans the77-metatile geographic gap.
- **Border Pines:** author a continuous northern woods/cedar-marsh winter
  section and readable southern snowline. Preserve lower open water and the
  actual Cut, bridge, grass and Surf contracts when choosing frozen surfaces.
- **North Shore:** author a substantial frosted upper gorge landscape while
  retaining open Lake Superior, real waterfalls, channels, bridges and story.
  Frozen headwaters must not create an unintended bypass of a native gate.

Exact extents and cells belong to each reviewed source contract. A numerical
cell quota does not establish visual quality or a complete winter section.

## Art and native behavior

`winter-art-v01.json` defines the initial additive vocabulary. Original source
files remain unchanged. Native snow, icy TALL_GRASS and striped ICE have distinct
appearance and collision. Snowy tree/canopy blocks retain blocking collision.
Any added mixed-edge art uses a new immutable recipe version; v01 stays frozen.

Native grass zero-index holes must survive recoloring so foreground priority
does not hide the player. Grass rustle colors follow the actual icy metatile;
unchanged green grass in the same derived tileset keeps its original cache and
colors. North Shore water/flower/waterfall animation must remain identical.

Native ICE already exists at permissions0x23/0x2b and preserves the direction
of the entering step; turning while stationary does not begin a slide. The
production Duluth slide/stop/Continue baseline passes in
`.local/ice-native-baseline-controller-v01.log`. It is evidence for the existing
mechanic, not proof that new winter geography is installed or playable.

Rust publication owns generated packs. Art is published first, then the reviewed
new region and substantial existing-route winter revisions. Only new modules
are lowered; existing map programs must remain exact. Source preimages, original
attribute/block mirrors, native grass footprints and inherited field-move
replacement rules require explicit preservation checks. Keep new map/group,
flag/trainer/spawn and existing seven-bit landmark bindings collision-free.

Native acceptance requires complete-map source review, meaningful negative
checks, whole written-pack preservation, actual controller slides/stops/returns,
encounter grass, original gates/rewards, real shelter return and Save/Continue.
Use headless native2D captures and inspect ordinary/weakest sections as well as
landmarks. Explicit local party/badge/RNG fixtures do not prove earned access.
Retain every original, rejected pass, conversion, crop, raw GPU readback and save.
