## Same-scale authentic comparison

Read-only source comparison and all retained originals:
`renders/ice/region-v01/authentic-player-comparison-v01/`.
`authentic-versus-region-native160.png` has actual160×144 player frames in each
cell; the2x sheet is only an explicit nearest-neighbor viewing conversion.
The authentic IcePath1F source uses its actual forced NITE palette and matching
CHRIS NPC palette. The region source uses its noon outdoor palette. Both use
unaltered native indexed geometry before the bounded winter recolor.

Personally reviewed whole IcePath1F, the complete new region, all12 region
player cameras and the same-scale6-frame comparison. The lower bank makes
snow/ICE readable within one camera; the upper cache presents a visible goal
and a real stop instead of a decoration in unbroken ice. Warm native wood on
the hut gives useful color contrast among snow pines. The south-bank elbow is
still visually simple: a short pine-bounded snow turn with no landmark. It is
narrow enough to read as travel rather than a plaza, but does not itself offer
an activity. The broad upper frozen bend has fewer pivot obstacles than native
IcePath and is primarily a river crossing; this is not a claim of equivalent
puzzle density. The139-step snow alternative is deliberately much longer than
the17-step glide and six-grass shortcut; actual controller pacing should be
reviewed before claiming the complete region is fun. Large dark forest blocks
are closed banks, not additional walking lawns or unexplored playable rooms.

Source composition acceptance, actual GPU pixel agreement and actual controller
behavior are separate receipts. No blanket Johto parity or complete native
region acceptance is asserted by this comparison.
