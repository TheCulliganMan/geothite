## First actionable recommendation: R12 Gunflint shelter/cache

Keep **16×12 metatiles**, `MnR12Cave1F` / `MN_R12_CAVE_1F`, exterior trigger66,23 and safe exterior66,24, native interior arrival/exit17,23, clear arrival17,22, Otta core5,5 and cache27,5 with `MAX_ETHER` / `EVENT_MN_R12_CAVE_ETHER`. Retain original scripts, item quantity, event identity, warp IDs, audio and all unrelated route/hut/story content. Do not invent a new badge, HM reward or compulsory story gate.

Recompose the existing floor into a winding entrance neck, a sheltered western rest shelf at Otta, a raised eastern cache shelf, and a lower connecting passage. Carve contours with actual cave wall-top/side/corner/bottom tiles; avoid a floor rectangle outlined by repeated loose boulders. Give the central rock rib an asymmetric silhouette and two genuinely different circulation paths: one short direct cross-passage, one stair-and-shelf loop that visibly returns to the entrance side. A narrow ledge shortcut is optional only if the native cave tileset supplies the real intended ledge collision and an ordinary ascent is proven; do not use decorative ledges to pretend the floor changed elevation.

Place native cave12 or30 at an actual rock-face interruption and join their walkable lanes to the two shelves. Exact quadrants:12=`[FLOOR,FLOOR,WALL,FLOOR]`,30=`[FLOOR,FLOOR,FLOOR,WALL]`,36=`[FLOOR,WALL,FLOOR,WALL]`. The wall quadrant must meet rock rather than a navigable blank side. These are ordinary steps, not warp entrances. Keep the existing exit07's real LADDER quadrant for the paired cave mouth. Do not replace stairs with STAIRCASE0x7a, which forces native Down movement. No imported outdoor tileset or recoloring is necessary inside a cave using its own authentic source art.

Design the cache branch as a discovery visible beyond a rock shoulder, with a purposeful approach and a useful return rather than a four-cell boulder detour. Otta's shelter should have clear standing/talking room and a protected small floor pocket, not occupy a vast square. Any spring should use real source water/bank collision with a readable accessible dry side; no Surf requirement unless a deliberate optional reward contract is separately approved. Avoid putting encounter grass indoors as a generic feature quota.

Render the whole room at native scale and at integer enlargement, plus160×144 entrance, Otta shelf, stair transition, cache approach and widest remaining plain-floor cameras. Compare to native Union Cave/Dark Cave/Mt Mortar stone-face and stair composition. Preserve every candidate/source/render. Root should approve one room's actual composition before this pattern is applied elsewhere.

Acceptance must include numeric per-quadrant paths with actors present; no accidental ladder/warp on the stair route; cache reachable before/after its pickup; both ordinary loop directions; every intended rock side rejects input; real exterior entrance/arrival/exit through `VisibleShellController::press`; Otta text ownership; actual item quantity/flag and repeat refusal; Save/Continue; and actual production native screenshots at matching state. Static reachability and an attractive whole render alone do not establish native playability.
