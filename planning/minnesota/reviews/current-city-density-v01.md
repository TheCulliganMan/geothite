## Findings and next place

St Paul is the stronger next whole-city composition target after the R10 pass.
Its native Kanto façades are coherent, but large connected areas of blank pale
paving and yellow brick separate many buildings. The northwest market and
central civic spaces need deliberate edges and shorter, readable approaches.
The native Saffron reference organizes its streets around clear building rows,
fenced boundaries and a central landmark. Copying its rectangular grid would
not give St Paul a local identity; use the reference to judge pacing, enclosure
and the amount of empty foreground in an ordinary player camera.

Minneapolis has distinct river crossings, St Anthony Falls, mixed native
façades, market canopies and many doors. Its ordinary streets still deserve
player-camera review for wide empty shoulders. Do not reopen the old house
seam defect from memory: the inspected current façades have complete joined
fronts. The gray horizontal-rung objects at metatiles(14,12), (19,26), (32,13)
and(38,23) are authored **benches**, not broken outdoor stairs. Private8e is the
exact native Park0e block, with WALL/WALL/FLOOR/FLOOR collision. Keep those
furniture objects out of blanket cave-stair replacements.
