## Proposed composition and actual play

Keep16×12 metatiles and the single original mouth. Replace the three parallel bars with a **seep basin, rising throat and split dry roof**. The lower approach should reveal an irregular, visibly bounded small water pocket on its eastern side; it is background geography beside an ordinary dry route, with no new HM gate. A fitted attached rock nose turns the dry path toward one clear native stone stair. Above that stair, the player chooses the sheltered western Otta chamber or the narrower northeast Max Ether discovery spur. The return from the upper lip offers a short native drop into the low basin plus the ordinary stair ascent. This gives arrival, destination choice and return distinct spatial roles within the same small room.

Provisional geometry envelopes, in **core tiles** (not new event anchors):

- Mouth/clear arrival stays exactly `(17,23)/(17,22)`. Approach vestibule approximately `[13,18,22,23)`, narrowing before it enters the basin; preserve the mouth metatile `(8,11)` exactly.
- Lower seep basin approximately `[10,13,28,21)`, with an eastern irregular water pocket roughly `[22,14,28,19)`. Fit shoreline into the cave wall so this does not become another central island ring. Keep its water graph strictly inside the room, visibly closed, and both destinations reachable without Surf. No new fishing table/service or ladder is promised.
- Rising throat around `[17,12,22,18)`: one clear native cave stair, useful two-way passage with its actual side-wall quadrant. Exact art orientation/approach coordinates must be chosen from the native sheet by sight and collision in the first render. The old four-stair quota and three-bar alignment are intentionally not retained as design constraints.
- Upper fork approximately `[9,7,23,14)`: fitted ceiling/attached wall noses, short2–4-core bottlenecks and modest passing pockets; avoid a broad flat terrace or a second long horizontal hallway.
- Otta's existing roof pocket remains around `[3,3,11,9)`, with original Otta `(5,5)`, east-facing, its complete four printed lines and script. Its approach should be visible from the upper fork, with native stairs only where the terrain actually changes level.
- Max Ether remains exactly `(27,5)` on a fitted northeast spur around `[21,3,30,10)`. The spur should reveal the reward beyond a turn without forcing it into the required Otta path.
- Keep a short two-lane native drop with legal landings and an ordinary return. Prefer preserving current lip around `(14,14)/(15,14)` if coherent; relocating geometry is allowed only with an explicit new review contract and real future controller probes. Existing story actors/programs are never moved to make a shape work.

The exact candidate edit boundary is the room's **interior metatiles `[1,1,15,11)`**; retain its outer closed perimeter and exact original mouth. Only room rows, purpose, candidate status and review-contract geometry annotations change. Do not change route exterior/layout, world/interiors registry, map identity/extent/attributes/palette, object records, text, items/quantity/flags, compiled programs, encounter/fishing definitions or native warps. No props/actors/rewards are added. All saved original epochs/renders remain immutable.
