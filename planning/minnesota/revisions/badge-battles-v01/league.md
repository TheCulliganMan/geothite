# Ely League and the eight postgame rematches

The Fen Waterline Intro and Field packs have identical parties for all four Elite Four members, Casey and all eight returning Gym Leaders. All 62 party slots belong to the active Minnesota roster. Every installed move has its full base PP. The League goes from Fern at Lv. 47–48 to Casey at Lv. 51–53. Each postgame invitation correctly gives the size and level range of its five-member team. ## The remaining teams

Fern's Dakotaskip can pass Agility from the last listed slot. Both packs enable competitive battle decisions. After a faint, the trainer evaluates every living replacement. During a turn, the trainer can also switch voluntarily. If Baton Pass is selected, the game evaluates every living recipient. The existing battle regression puts Fern's Dakotaskip against Agatot and verifies the pass back to Trapnack. With no living partner the AI prefers another usable move. Fern keeps her original order and her four-member challenge. Crystal's Koga opens with a Baton Pass Ariados. Polished Crystal's Sabrina instead gives Mr. Mime Baton Pass partway through her team. Both illustrate why access to a recipient matters.
