# St. Paul through Duluth

Brainerd has a useful answer to Bankmaw within walking distance of the clinic. A level-29 Reedraft caught east of town can drain Bankmaw's HP and restore its own. Hatchrush can hit the same Reedraft hard with Signal Beam, so there is still a reason to change partners. Before the battle, the optional entrance guide points players toward that catch while keeping the directions to Sivren and the open pools. After the Lake Badge, the guide points to the ranger at the fishing pier. Once the player has her report, the guide gives the north exit and the wording on the first Route 7 sign. The recipe guards the two original pages in both Fen Waterline editions. [Guide edit](lake-guide.json)

## What the five leaders teach

The five encounters bind 17 Pokémon to 68 full-PP move slots. All 68 are available at the partner's level or through its installed machine compatibility. Each leader demonstrates the awarded TM. The five teams have fixed levels, use the competitive planner and carry no trainer items. The examples below use successful, ordinary single hits with the conditions under the table.

## Returning to Sivren

- **Immediately after a loss.** From the clinic, walk east through Brainerd to Route 18. The city's east seam connects to `MnR18C00R00` at (0,10) and (0,11). The grass at (14,10) and (14,11) is reachable on foot in both directions. Reedraft occupies slot 0, or 30% of grass encounters, in the morning and daytime. It does not occupy a night slot. A normal level roll produced a level-29 Reedraft with Giga Drain, Tackle, Synthesis and Mega Drain for all three sample parties. Return to the gym door at (12,35). Inside, the optional guide at (7,11) faces the player and points out the catch through the pre-Champion branch. Lorla faces right from (4,8) and Bravel faces left from (13,5), each with a sight range of two. Sivren waits for conversation at (8,3).
- **A fresh catch.** A level-29 Reedraft's Giga Drain deals 71–84 of Bankmaw's 120 HP and can restore 35–42 HP when Bankmaw has that much HP left. Mega Drain deals 51–60. Dry Waterfall deals 27–32 of Reedraft's 85 HP. Against the ace, Signal Beam deals 83–98. This exercises the difference between the middle partner and the finisher without a grinding requirement or a gifted TM.
- **A partner from the road already traveled.** Velvawn occupies the 20% daytime grass slot at level 27 on the St. Cloud side of Route 6. Keep its level-21 Razor Leaf through its evolution at level 32. Shedstag's Grass/Ghost typing makes it immune to Torrout's Low Kick and Hatchrush's Quick Attack. Razor Leaf deals 51–60 of Torrout's 101 HP and 64–76 of Bankmaw's 120 HP. Torrout's dry Waterfall deals 22–26 of Shedstag's 90 HP, increasing to 33–39 in rain. Bankmaw has Bite for 47–56 and Hatchrush has Signal Beam for 36–43. These are useful matchups without a guaranteed sweep.
- **Historical stress case.** The November 8 earned-run log records a four-member party around Stembolt 26, Flossail 24, Rimepelt 32 and Chorip 24, with five Super Potions on the first attempt. The logged opposing team included Aurorill and Chorip. Today's Fen Waterline party has neither. As a separate matchup fixture, use those four player species and levels with current natural moves. Dry Waterfall from the current Torrout deals 108–128 of Rimepelt's 81 HP under the stated conditions. A clinic heal cannot solve that opening matchup. Running the same fixture again with the Route 18 Reedraft tests whether the available advice changes the result.

## Does the catch help on a retry?

Each cell gives **wins out of three battles**, followed by **opposing Pokémon knocked out across those battles** out of a possible nine. All three policies used the same five companions and seeds 1701, 4242 and 9001. The sixth Pokémon was a fresh level-29 Reedraft. The party started healed and chose the attack with the highest expected immediate damage. The third policy spent one ordinary switch turn to send in Reedraft when Bankmaw appeared. The opponent could attack or switch normally. [Retry regression](../../../../crates/crystal-assets/src/badge_battle_retry.rs) · [Party origins and levels](parties.md)

| Starter party | Original five | Reedraft sixth | Switch to Reedraft on Bankmaw |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pouchid | 0 wins, 1 KO | 1 win, 6 KOs | 1 win, 6 KOs |
| Kindlit | 1 win, 3 KOs | 1 win, 3 KOs | 1 win, 3 KOs |
| Birchit | 3 wins, 9 KOs | 3 wins, 9 KOs | 1 win, 7 KOs |

Reedraft in reserve helped the Pouchid party across all three seeds. Seed 4242 went from a loss to a win. The two other losses went from zero knockouts to one and two. The Kindlit party still lost before taking out even one opponent on seeds 1701 and 4242 under all three policies. All nine voluntary switches put Reedraft opposite Bankmaw for at least the next move decision, but spending that turn cost the Birchit party two wins. The local catch gives players a real matchup option. Automatically switching as soon as Bankmaw appears performed worse overall in this sample.
