# Castorock’s tracks in River Pocket Cave

The pool, pillar and riverbed each tell part of the story of a branch being dragged through Route 3’s cave. Previously the pillar and the riverbed used the exact same small rock, repeated 21 times in the room. The pool’s supposed grooves used a stone texture found on eight squares. The walker described the Strength boulder, but had the same directions after the player found a track or caught Castorock.

| Observation | Where to stand and look | What the cave now shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Waterline at (2,5) | (3,5), facing west | Two pale, parallel grooves cut across a slab above the western pool. Players can read them on their first visit. |
| Pillar at (20,4) | (19,4), facing east | Two cuts bend around the face of the northern stone. A bright splinter is caught in the turning groove. |
| Dry bed at (23,13) | (23,12), facing south | A forked branch lies across the original rounded stone beside the far pool. Its two arms join the same length of exposed wood. |

Two eastward pushes of the Strength boulder open the far bank. The walker by the entrance gives directions to an unread mark and names the Minneapolis mill foreman while the player lacks the HM. The foreman checks Avrin’s Current Badge. Players can read the three sites in any order. After the reveal, the walker points out Castorock near the far pool. After capture, the walker talks about the smooth branch. Looking at a familiar mark or talking to the walker preserves progress.
