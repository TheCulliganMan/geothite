# Minnesota crossings and stonework

## The two northern rocks

At **Mille Lacs**, the glacial erratic sits behind the same sapling. The new outline is lower on the left, with a small split across the stone and a mossy base. Four centre tiles use the native grey palette. The surrounding tiles share the grass palette with the grove. Search its eastern face for the Hard Stone.

At **Gunflint**, the slope returns to the trail's own cliff and snow tiles. The single Rock Smash boulder sits at the opening. It still gives the player a dry route to the loon lookout, and the ice remains a second way through.

## Water, fossils and the League

* On Route 15, a timber bridge connects the road toward Detroit Lakes. Water continues underneath to a sheltered landing with a hidden Lure Ball. [Shiner Narrows](../../routes/r15-shiner-narrows-v01/README.md)
* At Former Shores, the three fossil beds bend around grassy gaps. Follow the shell shapes across the old shorelines or fish the small side pond. [Former Shores](../../discoveries/former-shores-v01/README.md)
* At Ely, the recorder and open register share the head of a burgundy runner. The east gallery holds a family photograph of Jiglet. [Ely Hall of Fame](../ely-register-v01/README.md)
* In the Iron Range gallery, choosing Strength from a party Pokémon's menu now reaches the native field-move routine. Its badge and party requirements still apply.

## References and original artwork

Polished Crystal's Olivine pier uses short repeating boards to give the eye a clear line along the waterfront. The Pewter Museum lets players examine a small object at a larger scale. Pokémon Orange gives Valencia Port a recognisable fishing community. Crystal Legacy's southeast Whirl Island connects its seaward entrance and lower stair in a compact room. These projects informed the design review. All Minnesota layouts and new pixel patterns were authored for Northstar. [Polished Crystal’s Olivine pier](https://github.com/Rangi42/polishedcrystal/blob/f8e67fbd53ad7a99870d8ab87fd73612e6c6a679/screenshots/olivine-city.png) · [Pewter Museum](https://github.com/Rangi42/polishedcrystal/blob/f8e67fbd53ad7a99870d8ab87fd73612e6c6a679/maps/PewterMuseumOfScience1F.asm) · [Valencia Port](https://github.com/PiaCarrot/pokeorange/blob/5b4a95ec2d4638c7b8f125b6e1644390d52d4a5b/maps/ValenciaPort.asm) · [Whirl Island southeast](https://github.com/cRz-Shadows/Pokemon_Crystal_Legacy/blob/77e6b3859349698ad0a7a8a93cd27f78860d3e4a/maps/WhirlIslandSE.asm)

The new artwork uses 8×8, two-bit tiles with a single four-color background palette per tile. The outdoor maps use the native time-of-day palettes. Ely's indoor tileset has eight four-color RGB555 palettes. The published packs retain the game's character sprites.
