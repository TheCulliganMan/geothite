# Six partners for Ely

The challengers keep the five partners represented in the badge audit. They enter at levels 47, 46, 45, 46 and 45. The sixth partner joins from daytime grass on Route 13 at its authored level and keeps its four actual regional encounter moves. Each team remains together through the Champion. [Catch and training histories](../badge-battles-v01/parties.md) · [League party construction](../../../../crates/crystal-assets/src/ely_challenger_parties.rs)

| Starter | Sixth catch | Preparation at the Minneapolis workshop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pouchid | Fortigard 46, middle section, 10% | Restore Synthesis, Dig, Stun Spore, Magnitude and Headbutt. |
| Kindlit | Latchquill 45, southern section, 20% | Restore Bite, Stun Spore, Signal Beam and Leech Seed. Choirbog keeps its four moves. |
| Birchit | Pyropine 46, southern section, 10% | Restore Waterfall, Aqua Jet, Signal Beam, Dig, Razor Leaf and Psybeam. Voyagear keeps Drain Punch and Crunch. |

The percentages are shares of daytime encounters. From Ely's north edge at (24,0), the player enters the southern section at (34,31). Walkable ground leads to grass at (34,23) and crosses the north seam at x=32. In the middle section, ground connects the arrival at (32,47) to grass at (42,41). These are collision and connection checks on the selected Badge Battles intro pack.

The workshop apprentice charges ₽500 per forgotten move and checks the current species and level. Grand Marais Mart sells Gold Berries for ₽10 each. The preparation costs are ₽2,560, ₽2,060 and ₽3,060 in starter order, before Ely bag supplies. Each partner carries one berry, which restores up to 30 HP when HP drops below half. Construction assumes a healed party with DVs of 8 and zero stat experience. It models an attainable shopping budget, training history and catch outcome. It does not measure travel time, catch attempts or money earned on a particular save.
