# Bringing badge rewards to Ely

The earlier three starter parties bought medicines in Ely and recovered forgotten moves at the Minneapolis workshop. They never taught a badge TM. This comparison gives those same travelers a separate, attainable preparation history. They collected each leader’s machine, kept it until Ely and accepted the Brainerd ranger’s Surf HM after winning the Lake Badge. The fixture applies the actual gift commands, verifies the Pack quantities and teaches through the normal party moves menu’s underlying operation. The three numbers in each starter column follow seeds 1701, 4242 and 9001. Four wins reach Casey. Five defeat Casey and qualify the party for the Hall of Fame.

On Finder Lesson, Birchit’s machine-equipped party entered Casey’s room on seed 9001 with all six partners standing, two Revives, two Full Heals and no Hyper Potions. It won in 26 turns. Furrowbank finished on 99 of 136 HP and Voyagear on 21 of 150. The same seed with the earlier moves lost after knocking out three of Casey’s six. Pouchid’s machine-equipped team lost to Halden on all three attempts. Its earlier moves got it through Halden on seed 9001. The Birchit result establishes a full win on the older lineup with the same starting levels and shopping budget.

With Marshrack on Halden’s team, every starter reached the Champion on all three seeds with either moveset. Birchit’s seed-4242 party won its final match in 26 turns with three partners standing in both arms. Pouchid and Kindlit each lost all three of their final matches under both preparations. Across the three historical-party seeds, two entries reached Casey on each edition and neither edition produced a full win. These results establish a reachable championship and a more consistently reachable final match for the modeled parties. A larger range of player choices and party histories would be needed to estimate how often people win.
