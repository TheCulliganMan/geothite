# Champion rematches

The same six partners won all eight Gym rematches and Reed's challenge at St. Cloud Station. Both final Ely Return editions reproduced the nine Finder Lesson results at divider seed 1701, including every turn count, survivor count and prize. The archive comparisons also verified that each edition retained the nine teams and their battle scripts. [Native combat audit](../../../../crates/crystal-assets/src/postgame_rematch_trials.rs) · [Final archive comparison](check-pack.py)

Each fight began with a freshly healed level-57 team and one Gold Berry per partner. The player chose the highest expected damaging move available, used the first healthy reserve after a faint, and spent no Bag items or voluntary switches.

The comparison sets each partner's experience to the level-57 threshold and keeps its existing moves. Against this team on this seed, Saven's party fell in five turns. Varel, Brannic, Sivren and Reed each knocked out four partners. [Party histories](../ely-challenge-v01/parties.md)

In both Finder Lesson editions the original Gym reward comes before the first rematch. Each leader then requires the Champion title, accepts a refusal and offers another battle after the rematch reward. Reed appears after the restored survey, offers a battle to the Champion and stops challenging after his defeat.
