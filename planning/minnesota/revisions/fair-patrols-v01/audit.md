# Minnesota game audit

November 20, 2026 · Selected local Intro Fair Table edition

I would spend the next pass on five things a player can notice for themselves. Make fair trainers walk the midway and respect occupied squares. Give Route 15’s Cut trees a reason to exist. Make the Pokédex tell the truth about where a Pokémon lives. Show the worker the player helps rescue. Give the starters a second pose when they come out of their balls.

Three independent reviews covered geography, narrative and battles. I cross-checked the results against the selected game and reviewed the Pokédex, phone, credits, sprite animation and shops. The inventory covers 358 Minnesota maps, including 108 exteriors, 215 interiors, 30 cave or dungeon maps and five gates. The dialogue index covers 3,556 nonempty text entries connected to native scripts on 344 maps. Those counts describe the breadth of the scan. The scene readings, camera views and encounter examples are samples. This was a static review, with no controller play or new game build. The League numbers below are from earlier recorded trials whose relevant battle data matched this edition. [Edition and checksum](../fair-table-v01/intro-build.json)

## Changes made after the audit

The Fair Patrols editions address recommendations **1–5 and 17** from the Fair Table review below. Six trainers now walk or run and can spot the player from eight squares away. The same approach rule checks walls, people and bridge heights. Both written editions passed the map checks for 40 unobstructed eight-square sightings and access to all 32 vendor counters. Baker Ada’s Doughlin carries the 50 HP blueberry slice.

Route 15’s cabin sapling opens a 12-step shortcut to the hidden Ether. The long way is still 24 steps, and the pier and canoe landing keep their original rewards.

The Minnesota Pokédex now uses its own list, numbers and habitat map. Paddarray’s page names Winona and the Super Rod. Safari entries check whether the encounter surface actually exists. The starter bench remembers adoptions, and Tamarack recognizes a completed 225-species Pokédex in Ely. [Build instructions](README.md)

Fourteen recommendations remain open, along with the regional credits artwork in recommendation 5. The review describes the original Fair Table edition.

## Fix what players can catch the game getting wrong

1. **Keep the State Fair chaotic and make the whole crowd respect blockers.** Twenty of its 35 trainers turn and can spot you eight unobstructed squares away. Those twenty already check terrain and people. Fifteen use a fixed facing and a separate sight rule that skips the obstruction check. Their 29 starting sight squares are clear floor. The corner worth fixing is *after* someone moves. Ruth starts at (34,27) facing east. If she challenges a player at (37,27), she stops at (36,27) and stays there after a win. The player can walk around to (36,28). Eli at (36,26) can then spot the player through Ruth and attempt to step onto her square. Dale has a second example two aisles over. This sequence comes from the map and game rules. Use the same physical obstruction check for both sorts of trainer, preserving sight distances and automatic fights. Give several trainers the same kind of short walking loops already used by 28 fairgoers. All 35 trainers currently start on standing movement, and quick turns alone do not carry them through the midway. A clear eight-square challenge, a blocked stall or aisle, a guest crossing, and a second trainer after the first fight are the four acceptance cases. [Spotting and blockers](../../../../crates/crystal-core/src/world/session.rs#L3113) · [Approach movement](../../../../crates/crystal-bevy/src/bevy_shell/deterministic_session.rs#L2677) · [Fair layout](../../zones/state-fairgrounds.json)

2. **Give Route 15’s Lake Cabin a real Cut shortcut.** Cutting the tree at (49,10) gains exactly its own square. You can already walk around it in two steps. Cutting (48,18) gains the tree square and one empty dead end. Use one opening to connect the cabin to a recognizable shore landing. Remove the other or replace it with ordinary vegetation. Route 14’s Cut already saves 32 steps and Route 17’s saves 68. Nearby on Route 15, eight eastward jump tiles point straight into water. Keep the brown bank and correct its jump permissions wherever a landing is impossible. [Shiner Narrows water and walking routes](../../routes/r15-shiner-narrows-v01/README.md)

3. **Give the Pokédex AREA page fishing and tree habitats.** Paddarray is in Winona’s Super Rod table. Pathfang lives in Headbutt trees in the Boundary Waters. Neither appears in Minnesota’s ordinary grass, water or bounded grass tables. The AREA lookup uses those tables and two roaming slots, skipping fishing and Headbutt. Show town or route, method and relevant time on the area page. The useful answer for Paddarray is “Winona · Super Rod.” Check that a decorative pond or dry interior with an inherited table never becomes a claimed habitat. [Area lookup](../../../../crates/crystal-bevy/src/bevy_shell/pokedex.rs#L748) · [Regional habitat sources](../../pokemon/acquisition-v01/region-ecology-v01/habitats.json)

4. **Make the starter bench remember adoptions.** Winona’s research aide can give you a second starter. After taking it, its ball remains on the bench and says Tamarack will care for it. Update the matching bench slot on successful adoption. Declining or lacking party space should leave the ball available. Check all three starters, both adoption orders, and returning to the room after saving. [Starter bench](../../../../crates/crystal-assets/src/northstar_opening_revision.rs#L621) · [Aide’s handoff](../../../../crates/crystal-assets/src/minnesota_research_access.rs#L183)

5. **Let Minnesota celebrate a completed Minnesota Pokédex.** The regional research desk correctly ends at 225. Ely’s graphical Hall of Fame uses Professor Oak’s shared rating table and requires 249 for the perfect rating. A player who has collected every regional species gets a middling acknowledgment. Give Tamarack separate lines for 224 and 225, and let the credits feature the three regional starters and Kettlurk. Today the four animated credits strips feature Pichu, Smoochum, Ditto and Igglybuff. Keep the original credits attribution. [Ceremony](../../../../crates/crystal-bevy/src/bevy_shell/hall_of_fame.rs#L106) · [Credits cast](../../../../crates/crystal-bevy/src/bevy_shell/menu_rendering.rs#L3472)

## Make the campaign’s biggest moments land

6. **Pay off Captain Voss in Minneapolis.** The story names Voss in 31 text passages across eight maps. The cast identifies the ICE Enforcer as Voss, yet the enforcer you confront says Voss can come shut the door himself. Voss never speaks under his own name. I would keep the subordinate and let Voss answer him on the dispatch radio, then show what happens when his order is disobeyed. Ellis’s release still belongs to the player. [Cast entry](../../story.json#L2281) · [Showdown](../../story.json#L60148)

7. **Put the injured worker on screen at the Boundary Waters intake.** Ivy and Finn say they are supporting a third person by both arms, but the scene places no worker between them. Stage the three on the dry landing, let the party make room, and show all three reaching safety. Earlier, the radio asks for the pump times and then the script reads them for you. Let the player read the meter and send the observations. Keep the skipper’s remark about the exposed rock scrape. It makes the water level matter to someone. [Intake staging](../../../../crates/crystal-assets/src/northstar_rival_finale.rs#L196) · [Radio exchange](../../../../crates/crystal-assets/src/northstar_rival_finale.rs#L78)

8. **Give the Pokémon a recognizable entrance animation.** All 225 active species have front and back artwork. Every regional front animation program references exactly one picture, frame zero. In the same pack Crystal’s Chikorita references five distinct pictures. Start with Pouchid, Kindlit, Birchit and Kettlurk. Hand-author one characteristic movement each, such as a shift of weight or an ear turning toward the trainer. Preserve the accepted resting silhouettes and four-shade tiles. This finding concerns the Pokémon portrait, independently of the existing throw, slide, move and water animations. [Regional sprite program](../../../../crates/crystal-assets/src/minnesota_pokemon_modpack.rs#L539) · [Battle entry playback](../../../../crates/crystal-bevy/src/bevy_shell/battle_entry.rs#L880)

9. **Let a player identify the Team ICE floor from the elevator or warp landing.** Maintenance, Records, Operations and Transfer each use a 32×24 floor with similar blue speckled flooring and panel-lined aisles. Each has 10–12 warps. Keep the lettered warp puzzle, but put a distinctive landmark in the first screen of each arrival. A pipe junction belongs in Maintenance, filing racks in Records, a dispatch board in Operations and a loading lane in Transfer. Check every landing in an unlabeled native-size view. [Warp network](../../lairs/ice-headquarters/network.json)

10. **Let the city’s workplaces explain themselves when you walk in.** Minneapolis Arrival Info, the Boat House and the Couriers Desk share identical floor art and furniture. The Angler Home and Mill Foreman Home also use that layout. Preserve familiar Center and Mart plans. Fit three workplaces to their jobs first, with a public timetable, a boat-repair bench and a parcel counter respectively. Put the person who can help where the doorway naturally leads the eye.

11. **Give the Pokégear a Minnesota contact worth calling.** Winona gives the player the phone card. None of the 358 Minnesota map modules registers a phone contact, and the permanent entries are Mom and Elm. Give the player a number for Tamarack or the harbor dispatcher. After a week away, calling should identify the next person or place using the player’s real progress. Let a second, optional contact offer a rematch or a local wildlife tip. Crystal’s Joey is a reference for a contact with a purpose that changes over time. [Regional phone grant](../../../../crates/crystal-assets/src/northstar_opening_revision.rs#L136) · [Joey’s phone and rematches](https://github.com/pret/pokecrystal/blob/master/maps/Route30.asm)

## Give routes and side trips stronger identities

12. **Vary what tips the player off to hidden items.** All 59 rewards across 49 maps have a reachable search position and return path under the checked movement rules. Forty-nine are reachable on foot, seven by Surf and one each through Whirlpool, Rock Smash and Cut. The weakness is recognition. Routes 3 and 18 repeat a flower-patch clue, Routes 6, 7, 17 and 19 repeat a forest-edge pocket, and several caves repeat a lone brown stone. Use an observation such as an angler snagging something off the Duluth pier, or a feature a player recognizes from the opposite side of the water. Keep the one-time rewards and regional shoreline art. [Entry and return checks](../lake-observations-v01/check-hidden.py#L149)

13. **Give breakable rock a consistent visual meaning.** The two Rock Smash rocks, on Gunflint Route 12 and in the Route 10 cave, use the same sprite as 16 inert Safari campsite stones. Show the campsite rings as low ground stones or tent pegs. Keep the fractured rock for an actual break. Minnesota has no Rock Smash encounter tables. One optional rock-dwelling species at an existing fracture would reward curiosity and give that site a second purpose. [Gunflint location](../../../../crates/crystal-assets/src/northstar_northern_rockfall.rs#L59)

14. **Keep a dependable low-level patch near Winona.** Returning to Route 1 with a badge and three level-45 partners can produce the same Nibbin slot at level 2 or levels 42–46. A player trying to catch a beginner or train a newly adopted starter cannot tell which they will get. Make the verge nearest town a reliable nursery. Let a farther patch hold the encounters that scale up for returning teams, and distinguish it with an existing landmark. [Level selection](../../../../crates/crystal-assets/src/wild_level_refuges.rs#L26)

15. **Give dawn two or three regional signatures.** All 95 default positive-rate grass habitats and all 36 bounded grass zones use identical species in the same positions in morning and daytime. In the Crystal data, National Park changes from morning Ledyba to daytime Sunkern. Give a marsh and a northern woodland a dawn visitor that fits the habitat. Keep another way to obtain it for players who cannot play in the morning. [Regional habitat design](../../pokemon/acquisition-v01/region-ecology-v01/habitats.json) · [Crystal’s encounter tables](https://github.com/pret/pokecrystal/blob/master/data/wild/johto_grass.asm)

16. **Give distant route trainers partners that tell you where you are.** Boomruff appears in 31 of 293 trainer party slots on the numbered routes and visits 19 of the 20 roads. Keep it as a familiar early companion. On a few northern or shoreline teams, give the trainer a local partner whose move or behavior fits the place. The gym students already demonstrate the idea with 25 different species across 25 team slots. [Trainer and battle plans](../badge-battles-v01/league.md)

## Make optional battles, prizes and visits earn their place

17. **Let the fair baker demonstrate held food.** Ada says her partner gets the first slice. Neither Doughlin nor Oatlet actually holds food. Across 303 ordinary trainers, all 638 party slots are empty-handed. Give one of Ada’s partners a real snack that it can eat below half HP, once. That teaches a player precisely what giving a blueberry slice to their own Pokémon does. The selected edition already has working use, give, healing, consumption and save behavior for the regional food. [Ada’s team](../../zones/state-fairgrounds.json#L4258) · [Food effects and previous checks](../fair-table-v01/README.md)

18. **Focus first-League balancing on Casey.** In the recorded nine consecutive challenges, all nine prepared teams reached Casey, while one won the circuit. All three starts were represented across three fixed seeds, with the same shopping budget. A human win rate would need a different sample. Compare two tightly scoped options first, a better opportunity to prepare for Casey’s lead or an adjustment to one of Casey’s coverage moves. Preserve Halden’s preparation break. Separately check Saven’s five-turn rematch loss against several teams before changing it on the strength of one matchup. [First challenge](../ely-return-v01/README.md#L9) · [Rematch evidence](../ely-return-v01/postgame.md)

19. **Give the Game Corner a middle prize worth aiming for.** The selected Minneapolis counter really gives out the Coin Case, sells coins, runs two games and awards six prizes. Quick Claw costs 300, Rare Candy 500 and PP Up 1,000. All three offensive TMs jump to 5,500 each, equivalent to 110,000 in cash if every coin is purchased. Add one distinctive 1,500–2,500 coin prize a player might earn on an early visit. Keep the three premium TMs and the cheap held item. [Counters, machines and coin exchange](../../../../crates/crystal-assets/src/minnesota_game_corner.rs#L35)

20. **Give the State Fair its own sound.** The main Fairgrounds map and Winona both select New Bark Town’s music. The Fairgrounds has 255 placed people, 32 stalls and 35 trainers. A short bustling midway theme with a recognizable refrain would mark arrival as clearly as the crowd does. Use the quieter home tune for the return to Winona. Check the transition through the entrance and that normal map music resumes after a battle. [Fairground plan](../../zones/state-fairgrounds.json) · [Selected edition](../fair-table-v01/intro-build.json)

## What already earns its place

- Mom putting out one bowl at the beginning and a bowl for every muddy partner after Ely makes the campaign feel like it happened to a family. Sera worrying about Finn’s ladder and later teasing him about his lunch tin does the same for a town. Use those scenes as models for future dialogue. [Opening](../../story.json#L2665) · [Homecoming](../../story.json#L69739) · [Sera](../../story.json#L52957)
- Ordinary trainers initiate battles on sight, and the eight leaders wait for you to speak. All eight leaders have access to their prize move. Brainerd’s open lake and the Shiner Narrows canoe passage already make the case for keeping open water and readable brown banks. [Brainerd](../../gyms/brainerd-lakeside-v01/README.md) · [Shiner Narrows](../../routes/r15-shiner-narrows-v01/README.md)
- The exterior connection and warp graph reaches all 108 exteriors. The active roster, regional Pokédex membership and final research threshold agree at 225 species across 99 families. Every family has a potential acquisition source and evolution fills out the roster. Catching every species and walking every route would require a different kind of review. [Acquisition audit](../../tools/audit_species_acquisition.py)
- Crystal’s Union Cave makes a defeated trainer’s remark about a Friday sound an invitation to come back. Polished Crystal gives certain evolutions a meaningful location and an alternate method. The useful lesson for Minnesota is to let its specific places and people teach players something they can act on later.
