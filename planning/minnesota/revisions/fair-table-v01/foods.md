The butter roll costs the same as a pretzel and restores the same 20 HP. A cardamom bun matches a serving of mini donuts, as does the blueberry slice. A bowl of mushroom and wild rice soup matches the Fair's 60-HP root beer. If your Pack is full, the hostel cook keeps your roll wrapped for another visit.

## Drinks and berries

Fresh Water, Soda Pop, Lemonade and Moomoo Milk heal 50, 60, 80 and 100 HP respectively when used from the Pack. The bottled root beer at the Boundary Waters cabin also restores 60 HP from the Pack.

At the St. Paul berry counters, a Berry, Berry Juice and a Gold Berry restore 10, 20 and 30 HP respectively. They work from the Pack and when held. The specialty berries keep their own effects. Mint wakes a sleeping Pokémon, Bitter clears confusion in battle, Przcure clears paralysis, Psncure clears poison, and Ice Berry treats a burn. A held Mysteryberry activates when a move runs out of PP. It restores 5 PP, or 1 PP for Sketch.

## Fair encounters

Four foods can draw a level-10 Pokémon when purchased at the stands listed above. Cheese curds attract Curdlet, hotdish attracts Totlit, a cookie pail attracts Doughlin, and kettle corn attracts Pipcorn. With a usable Pokémon in the party, each qualifying purchase has a one-in-five chance, including a purchase of multiple portions. The trainer keeps the food.
