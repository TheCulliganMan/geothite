# The Rochester court and Mankato quarry

Rochester’s guide offers a quick stair past Elvik and a longer walk around the court. In Badge Trails, a player could walk sideways onto Varel’s platform from (14,6) without using either stair. The Gym Walk layout brings both flights to the front corners and joins the upper sides to their steel posts. The west corner retains its potted plant beside the bench. From the public entrance at (12,15) to the court at (12,5), the right stair takes 14 steps and crosses Elvik’s sight at (14,7). The quiet west route takes 24 steps, or 26 if Tavel stepped into the center lane to challenge the player.

In Mankato, players could walk through the west wall’s shadow at (1,7) and ascend beside the upper steps. The first jump also started from a patch of pale outdoor dirt set into golden sandstone. A rock shoulder now closes the gap beside the stairs. Both upper drops use the plateau’s sandstone and the quarry’s lower rock edge. After Jorel loses, the east gate opens onto its actual staircase at (12,7) and (13,7). The initial drop at (6,6) lands at (6,8); a player who wants to retreat can reach the lobby at (6,17) in eight moves before facing Jorel.

Crystal’s Violet Gym has a raised path whose visible footprint determines the walk to Falkner. On the first floor of Polished Crystal’s Blackthorn Gym, the two climbable connections at (1,7) and (7,9) use ladders. The three return squares for drops from upstairs are plain floor. These rooms informed the distinction between upper surfaces, solid faces, stair treads and lower landings.
