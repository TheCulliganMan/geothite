# Mina’s date and Sera’s radio call

At St. Paul Union Depot, Ellis’s cracked coupling gets the miner talking. His defeat and Reed’s defeat are separate events. Mina then offers the date her pump ring split. She keeps the slip if the player declines. Until the player has seen the Brainerd ranger, Mina’s directions to the pier still make sense.

A player can already have the ranger’s log. The ranger asks for the Lake Badge and describes her boat hanging from its rope. Mina now offers her date to compare with that reading, or discusses the log if she has already given the date. After the survey is restored, she still talks about Finn phoning her. No new trip to Brainerd or Ellis is required.

## The two St. Cloud visits

| What has happened | What the player finds |
| --- | --- |
| Reed has left St. Paul; Finn is still below | Reed and Sera stand in the north road. Both south approach lanes share the scene. Declining leaves Sera there, and she asks the player to stand with her if they return. |
| The player stands beside Sera | Sera thanks the player face to face. She crosses to the east lane, walks south to the cross street, turns east around the trees and walks north to the station. |
| The player leaves town and comes back | Sera is on the same forecourt square. The station door is one step west and one step north. The street and doorway remain walkable. |
| The clerk has reached Nell | The clerk directs the player back outside. Sera learns that Nell heard the call. Repeat visits retain the report. |
| Finn is safe before the player helps Sera | Sera waits beside the station and responds to Finn’s rescue. Road entry records no intervention or radio call. |
| The water is restored | Sera talks about Finn returning with Nell. Reed is inside the station. He offers his six-partner match only after the player becomes Champion, then remembers the result. |

The old return left Sera inside the tree corridor at (24,9), across a stand of pines from the station she named. From the square south of her, getting to the radio clerk took 35 steps. The same walk from her new spot at (38,6) takes 14. The refusal previously sent the player to ask the Brainerd ranger for a winch, although the ranger had no such interaction.

A late arrival can happen without unusual inputs. Before challenging Reed in St. Paul, there is a dry road through Minneapolis and St. Cloud to the Brainerd ranger. Entering Brainerd registers its Fly destination. St. Cloud’s Wind Badge and HM Fly are available independently of Sera. A player who revisits Brainerd this way can pass the shut-down drill and restored-water milestones before intervening in St. Cloud.
