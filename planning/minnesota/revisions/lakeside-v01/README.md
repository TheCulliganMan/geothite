# Pokémon Northstar · Lakeside

The lake is Brainerd’s gym floor. From the cedar entrance posts you can see pale water stretching north to Sivren’s beach. Lorla has drawn a starting line on the west sandbar. Bravel’s red fishing float sits just off the east bank. Step into either trainer’s sight to battle, or wade straight to the leader.

## Twenty design decisions

| # | Change | What the player can read or do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Give shallow water its own light value. | Follow the eight-step channel from the entrance to Sivren. |
| 2 | Put sand beneath every person’s feet. | Read the four occupied positions as actual shore. |
| 3 | Put Lorla’s starting line into the sand. | Recognize her practice lane before the battle. |
| 4 | Place the fishing float offshore. | Find Bravel’s casting position from the opposite bank. |
| 5 | Beach a canoe behind the leader. | Recognize Sivren’s destination on the small screen. |
| 6 | Keep both students’ sightlines on side approaches. | Challenge a swimmer or angler without talking. The leader waits for you. |
| 7 | Let Varel’s Nibbin use Headbutt. | Learn what the first gym prize does while Crookit retains its quick finish. |
| 8 | Give Torven’s Sodcalf Mud-Slap. | Experience the accuracy drop before deciding who should learn the TM. |
| 9 | Give Minneapolis’s opening Pipulse ThunderPunch. | See the reward early. Sparkveil now has a lighter Electric attack later in the fight. |
| 10 | Put Steel Wing on Brannic’s Patchebee. | A winged regional Pokémon demonstrates the prize. |
| 11 | Give Duluth’s Lutebrisk Icy Wind. | Discover its Speed effect in the badge fight. |
| 12 | Make two prized TMs suit early regional catches. | Seven more species learn ThunderPunch and ten learn Steel Wing. |
| 13 | Give Rochester’s timekeeper his own silhouette. | Recognize Tavel’s running pose and stopwatch when the field encounter cuts to battle. |
| 14 | Tell players the onward road as well as the optional HM trip. | Varel points west to Mankato. Torven points north to Minneapolis. Each also names the return trip for the HM. |
| 15 | Give the Minneapolis guide a believable wrong turn. | Distinguish the northern bluff road to St. Cloud from the east road to the St. Paul gym. |
| 16 | Let the ranger and observer describe separate evidence. | Compare a boat rope hanging over mud with the rung where a leaky boot first took on water. |
| 17 | Give the marker a return address. | Collect the readings at Itasca and take them east to Bemidji’s field officer. The west trail sign now describes the actual fork. |
| 18 | Name the first missing badge at registration. | Get the leader and city for each of the eight possible refusals. |
| 19 | Remember the champion’s first supper. | Mom celebrates the whole party, heals it, and has a shorter conversation on later visits. |
| 20 | Put the approved regional sprites into the game. | The battle, party and field presentations can draw on the same 225 species already pictured in the Northwoods Pokédex. |

## Comparisons

Jasmine’s first request at Olivine Lighthouse names Cianwood’s pharmacy. Returning with the medicine changes both her behaviour and Amphy’s. In Slowpoke Well, Kurt and the rescued Pokémon give the outcome a presence outside the villain battle. Those two scenes informed the Itasca evidence trail and the two versions of Mom’s homecoming. [Olivine Lighthouse](https://github.com/pret/pokecrystal/blob/3bc8daa4173e96a7f4011dad3922eb6fa5dad5c6/maps/OlivineLighthouse6F.asm) · [Slowpoke Well](https://github.com/pret/pokecrystal/blob/3bc8daa4173e96a7f4011dad3922eb6fa5dad5c6/maps/SlowpokeWellB1F.asm)

Crystal Legacy’s Bugsy gives Ledian Reflect and coverage, then Scyther brings Fury Cutter. Northstar uses that distinction between lead and finisher for Rochester and Minneapolis. Trainer species, levels, parties and art remain Minnesota’s own. [Crystal Legacy trainer parties](https://github.com/cRz-Shadows/Pokemon_Crystal_Legacy/blob/77e6b3859349698ad0a7a8a93cd27f78860d3e4a/data/trainers/parties.asm)

Polished Crystal’s Kingdra keeps its long snout readable from both directions. Pokémon Orange makes Blastoise’s glasses recognizable from behind. Their silhouettes and compact field frames were references for checking Minnesota’s accepted front, back and walking views, and for drawing Tavel’s stopwatch and raised sneaker.
