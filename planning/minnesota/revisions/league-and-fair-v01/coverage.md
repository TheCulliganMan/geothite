## Route 18 Shore Watch

From Brainerd’s east road at (63,24) or Mille Lacs’s west road at (0,12), the reed volunteer at (15,33) and meadow volunteer at (31,9) are in `MnR18C00R00`. The beach volunteer at (11,17) is in `MnR18C01R00`. Their respective adjacent talking squares are (14,33), (31,10) and (10,17). All six orders reach the ranger at (27,23) on the western map and allow a return to either entrance with standing actors in place. All three reports can be collected before the Lake Badge. Repeating a report sets the same flag. [Route coordinates and quest scripts](../../routes/MN_R18.json)

The 256 ranger cases vary badge, prior reward, eight possible report subsets, rod held and whether the Key Items pocket accepts the gift. A missing report or badge defers the award. An Old Rod still qualifies for one Good Rod. An existing Good or Super Rod completes the errand without giving another. Refusing the item for lack of space preserves all reports and leaves the receipt unset. Once a slot opens, the script grants the rod once and acknowledges later visits. Only Key Items capacity affects the grant. The archive contains 23 distinct other keys excluding Good and Super Rod, against 25 slots. A refusal was checked as a script condition. Reaching that inventory in ordinary progression was not established.
