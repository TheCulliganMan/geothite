# The two roads to Ely

From the Duluth Gym doorway, the short walk takes Route 11 and turns west through Ely Shore Pass. The Boundary Waters route leaves the south checkpoint and crosses all three sections of Route 13. Ely’s lodge has two working doors. The registrar checks all eight badges and the restored survey before offering to start a run. The town and its services are accessible before registration.

The static walking check finds a 243-step dry path from the gym return square to the western lodge apron in each direction. The walk between the Boundary Waters checkpoint and the Ely border takes 195 steps each way. Both Route 13 lanes cross into town and return at the same column. The final sign, Tomas’s four-square sightline, and the separate Cut approach to Ivy and the Revive are intact. [Walking and archive checks](check-approach.py)

## What the player can see

In Crystal, Route 26 places its League sign beside the reception door. The surrounding cliffs frame the approach, and the sign and warp agree. On the earlier Minnesota route the last opening looked six squares wide, but only its eastern two crossed into town. Sixteen more squares of snow formed a strip enclosed on all sides, and a wall of trees cut off the small Headbutt grove. The final approach narrows between the snowy pines before the player reaches town. On the first Ely square, bare gravel appears in the middle of the path while snow stays deeper under the western trees. The road and snow use their own installed textures. Each 8×8 tile uses at most four colors at morning, noon and night.

The western turn beside Tomas now reaches two short frosted pines. The grove uses the same pine crowns as the snowy Headbutt trees on Route 8 and the same snow as the path below. Both trees still draw from Route 13’s original level-46 encounter table.
