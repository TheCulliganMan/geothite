# Inside the Ely League

Casey offers the returning Champion a rematch. In both Fen Clearings editions, saying no kept the offer available but left the player in a room with two unusable exits. Both south squares at (4,23) and (5,23) carried a return destination on ordinary floor, which cannot start a warp. Veil had a second version of the problem. Her west exit at (4,17) was ordinary floor and the eastern half at (5,17) transported the player immediately upon stepping onto the single stair.

Both rooms now have a visible, two-square south threshold. A player can stand on it, turn back or take one more step south to leave. Either side leads to its matching lane in the previous room. Veil uses the same narrow doorway treatment as the other three Elites. Casey’s red runner keeps its original columns and meets a thin threshold made from the room’s own tile art.

Crystal closes Will’s door behind the player with a visible wall and an earthquake. Polished Crystal does the same at Lance’s entrance. Ely’s registrar explicitly allows withdrawing, and Casey lets a returning challenger decline. Giving this room a working exit makes those choices compatible with the floor plan. | Player action | Installed behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Approach the registrar | Both lobby landings connect to (5,4). From there the player can speak across the counter. The desk identifies the first missing badge, then the next unfinished survey handoff. Registration requires eight badges, a restored survey and an affirmative answer. |
| Take on the League | Fern, Flint, Veil, Halden and Casey each guard the next northern doorway. Winning opens both lanes. On reentry the door reads the number of wins in the current run. Speaking to a defeated member cannot start a second battle or award a second prize in that run. |
| Manage the party between rooms | The four Elites bring four partners apiece. Casey brings six. Base victory prizes without a multiplier are ₽2,304, ₽1,568, ₽1,568, ₽2,448 and ₽5,300. Room entry and Continue preserve HP, PP, carried items and money. |
| Withdraw or lose | A retreat within the five rooms preserves the run. Reaching the lobby withdraws the entry. All five chambers are indoors, so Dig, Escape Rope, Fly and Teleport cannot substitute for a working door. Registration sets Ely’s clinic as the blackout destination. The clinic’s entry callback clears the run and win count and retains previous title and survey progress. |
| Take the title and return | Five wins in the active run unlock recording through the attendant or the register. The Hall records the party, requests its presentation and ends the run. Continue after the credits uses Ely’s clinic at (3,5). A new registration starts at Fern. An existing Champion who declines Casey can return to Halden or speak to Casey again. |

The four Elites pay ₽7,888 and the full course pays ₽13,188 at ordinary rates. Victory uses the final party slot’s level, so Veil’s level-49 closer determines her prize even though another partner reaches 50. The game pays four shares and can send one of those shares to Mom if the player enables savings and her account has room. An Amulet Coin holder sent into battle doubles the prize. Pay Day credits the wallet separately. Each balance caps at ₽999,999, so the displayed award can exceed the money the wallet actually gains. `check-league-rooms.py PARENT [CANDIDATE]` reads the compiled collision, warp destinations, parties, scripts and native tiles. The parent-only mode reproduces the original defect. The pair comparison verifies the two room changes and the single appended native block. It covers all 510 incomplete-badge states with or without survey completion, 128 survey handoff combinations, 240 room decisions and both lanes of each of the five forward and return doors.

The `league_journey_` library tests use the pack selected by `MN_LEAGUE_JOURNEY_PACK`. One exercises the ordinary directional movement and warp path on all four repaired squares. The other runs the Hall’s actual record command with two Minnesota partners. The existing `lake_observations_five_league_doors` test runs entry and Continue callbacks with a wounded party, spent PP, money and medicine. These checks cover the state and destination behind the ceremony. No interactive playthrough or credits presentation was used for this review.
