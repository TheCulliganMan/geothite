# Next bounded exterior study: west waterfront

This records the next design scope after native review of the northeast pilot.
It does not apply another citywide template or promise new gameplay services.
Use the actual published successor of the current pilot as the next parent;
retain the original forty-front inventory as before evidence.

The installed interiors and their actual scripted services are authoritative.
The Boat House is not currently a boat rental, Angler Home does not currently
award a rod, and the Lake Cabin is not a paid inn. Do not add misleading shop,
healing or transport frontage. Dispatcher House must remain a legible family
home with its current story and paired room return.

The current exteriors use cyan brick, oversized vertical blue caps and near
identical window/door assemblies. Boat House and River Inn also repeat the
same two-storey window band despite different locations and roles.

For the next source loop, study these four **together** with their real banks,
bridge, streets and doors, then choose distinct silhouettes. A low timber
waterfront building can suit the cabin; the Boat House can have a broader,
lower roof and visible public-facing frontage; Angler Home should read as a
small inhabited house rather than another workshop; Dispatcher House should
read as a compact family home near the office. Choose native donor recipes
after looking at the accepted pilot in the production renderer. Do not copy the
same new diamond gable onto all four.

Keep the existing door quadrants, passive returns, footprint collision,
streets, rails, banks, room identities and story. Native boats, extra entrances,
porches or shoreline platforms require an explicit playable contract; art
alone must not imply an inaccessible destination. Render the complete 40×32
city and the full west waterfront, plus same-position 160×144 player views of
each house and the ordinary walk between them. The native art append must again
prove old logical bank pixels and all palette banks unchanged.

The large masonry façades should be handled separately. The Department Store
is 10×14 core tiles; Grain Exchange and Community Library are 10×8. Their flat
repeated courses need composition appropriate to their real activities and
floor counts. They are not accepted merely because native Saffron also has
large masonry buildings.
