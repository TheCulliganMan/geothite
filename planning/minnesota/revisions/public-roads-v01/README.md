## Text and source scope

Every retired checkpoint's permit/refusal texts become short public directions.
Falls staff invite reports rather than require them for passage. The Minneapolis
upper-pool notice, Duluth north sign, five obsolete rail-denial texts and St Paul
clerk match the open service. League's survey refusal now refers to completing
the rescue before registration, not a supposedly closed public trail. All use
authored one/two-line pages of at most18 native glyphs.

Only new source leaves are owned here:

- `crates/crystal-assets/src/northstar_public_roads.rs`
- `crates/crystal-assets/src/bin/pack_northstar_public_roads.rs`
- `crates/crystal-bevy/src/bevy_shell/tests/dialogue_public_roads.rs`

No shared registration or frozen story file was edited. The earlier proposed
story-cache patch was withdrawn after the owner established exact row-multiset
and raw-index equivalence. It must not be applied. This road publisher parses
changed raw scripts through the existing canonical Rust finisher; array order
is not a new gameplay behavior. Split geometry/actor aliases are restored exactly,
and the final whole-data comparison permits only map script/coordinate/callback
changes. All art, audio, terrain, actors, existing flags, teams and rewards remain.
