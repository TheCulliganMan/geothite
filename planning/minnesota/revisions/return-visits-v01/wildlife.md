# Fixed Pokémon and their cries

At Rochester’s clinic, the Pokémon at (1,6) looks and reads as Loobit. Its interaction in both Trail Discoveries parents selected Gillpup’s recorded cry, `CRY_MN_310`. Town Returns selects Loobit’s own recording, `CRY_MN_040`. The historic object label `MN_ARC_GILLPUP` still identifies the actor in saves. It disappears after survey restoration. The two other actors with Gillpup in their script names, at Winona (25,11) and Leech Lake (31,26), visibly depict Chorip, name it in their dialogue and do not play a cry.

The regional inventory uses the game’s runtime map groups beginning with `MINNESOTA`. Their member names match the `Mn` map-name set exactly in both editions. That covers 355 Field rooms and 357 Intro rooms, including the route chunks that use mixed-case map constants. Nineteen clinic stairs also connect to the shared Crystal Pokémon Center upstairs. Its three receptionists and officer have no creature actor.

Running the checker normally requires the corrected cry and the same consistency across the rest of the region. This compares archive records and graphic assets.
