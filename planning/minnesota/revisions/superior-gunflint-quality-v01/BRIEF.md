## Direction

Remove unnecessary connector travel. North Shore must meet Grand Marais
simply and directly; both remain warm and on the same Lake Superior. The latest wording identifies the straight approach from North Shore
to town. The selected candidate removes twenty metatiles from that approach,
leaving only a four-metatile coastal bend. It also retires the separate long
snowy inland Neck, so neither corridor survives as unnecessary travel.

Shorten Gunflint substantially. The64×56 extent is not a preservation rule.
The current author is composing a32×32 candidate around its useful features;
that is a working design size, not an arbitrary universal target. Native
identities, story, services, trainer/reward identities and meaningful Cut access
must survive, with explicit relocated anchors and paired room returns.
The old forty-ICE-quadrant and4,586-seasonal-cell counts describe history;
they must not force a poor new layout or accidental isolated pocket.

## Quality problems to fix

- Long orthogonal tunnels between repeated pine walls dominate ordinary views.
- Gunflint's lakes have overly rectangular outlines and too few useful shore
  approaches. Large rectangular cliff platforms consume space without creating
  interesting traversal.
- Whole-route space is much larger than the activities justify. Shorten trips
  between actual destinations rather than add more bends to the old corridor.
- The winter side bay lost its dry access after the previous ice conversion.
  Recompose its access deliberately; don't preserve an accidental restriction
  just because a Surf fixture can reach it.

Use authentic Route42 and Route44 terrain/player views already retained under
`renders/route-visual-audit/9cb8936dbd1a/` as references for shoreline structure,
varied tree groups, useful open ground and readable destinations. Winter must
remain a substantial coherent place, with native snow pines/cold grass,
intentional sliding ice and ordinary dry stops and returns.

Render the whole new Gunflint and complete coastal arrival together with town.
Inspect the least interesting ordinary stretches as well as landmarks. Show
actual changes in player views. Don't substitute prop scattering, a new name,
a small crop or only passing collision tests for a better composition.
