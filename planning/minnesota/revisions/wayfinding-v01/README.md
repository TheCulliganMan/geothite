# Minnesota routes and underground passages

| Place | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Route 1, Fossil Bluff | A low cliff lip below the fossil drops into the grass. The uphill path still winds around the bluff. |
| Route 2, Prairie Farm Road | The wheel track reaches a bank overlooking the lower wheat field. Jump there on the way back from the farmhouse. |
| Route 4, river park | The opening in the fence joins the picnic lawn to the riverside path in either direction. |
| Route 10, Iron Descent | Cut the sapling to reach a fork. The western branch holds a Sharp Beak. The eastern brow drops onto the ore bench. |
| Route 10, Survey Gallery | The surveyor gives you TM08. Smash the gallery fracture, search the seam for a Star Piece, and drop back to the spring. |
| Route 12, Gunflint Trail | Fragments below the cliff point to a break in Raven Rock. Break it to reach a dry ridge above the frozen basin. Both crossings reach the loon lookout. |
| Route 18, Mille Lacs Shore | A Cut sapling opens a grove around a rounded glacial erratic. Search its east face for a Hard Stone. A ledge leads back to the marina. |
| Iron Range mine | Ladders rise and recessed hatches descend at seventeen shafts. Rails identify Haulage, fans identify Ventilation, and the discharge pipe leads toward the pump. |
| Caves on Routes 3, 13 and 20 | Follow a gravel bar to the Potion, choose a bank on the way to the healer, or Surf across Route 20's underground waterway. |
| Routes 11, 14 and 20 | A mooring ring, a hollow at the end of a snow trail, and a fissured frost boulder mark the three hidden finds. |
| Team ICE cargo basement | Two lookouts turn across the crate aisles and challenge a player who enters their line of sight. |
