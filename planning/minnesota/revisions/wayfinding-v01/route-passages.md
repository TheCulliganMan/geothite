# Prairie Farm Road and Mille Lacs Shore

On Route 2 the gap south of the farmhouse continues the plowed lane to a low bank. Jump into the lower wheat field and turn west toward Mankato. The climb from the field still goes past the bridge and around the hedge. The old yard-to-field walk took 30 directional inputs in either direction. The new descent takes five. From the farmhouse front walk to the western road the return falls from 44 inputs to 27. The opposite walk remains 44. Counts describe static paths with current people in place. Crystal’s Route 46 provided a reference for letting the rocky lip and grass landing show how a return trip differs from the approach. [Route 46 map](https://github.com/pret/pokecrystal/blob/master/maps/Route46.blk)

On Route 18 a break in the trees beside the west road reveals a small sapling. Cut it and enter a grassy clearing with a glacial erratic. Searching the stone from its eastern face grants one Hard Stone. Past the flowers a low ledge returns to the marina. The road, the open water, and the marina cannot reach the stone before Cut. A player already inside can get out even if the sapling has regrown. Crystal’s Ilex Forest provided the native scale reference for recognizing a single sapling amid full-size trees. [Ilex Forest map](https://github.com/pret/pokecrystal/blob/master/maps/IlexForest.blk)

Both designs use the released regional design pack as their parent. The [authored placements](route-passages.json) use metatile coordinates. The Route 2 publisher adds three native farm-bank variants with the existing hop and shoulder permissions. Route 18 uses the installed Minnesota sapling, native replacement and existing lake tileset. No former standing or water tile becomes an obstacle on either route.
