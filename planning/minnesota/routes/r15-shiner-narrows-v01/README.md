# Shiner Narrows on Route 15

The road walker directs visitors across a bridge to Detroit Lakes. In the released Wayfinding map the southern crossing was lawn. The main lake had 328 connected water squares. The puddle directly south of the road could only be reached by getting out and launching again.

A timber span now carries the road over the outlet. Its upstream and downstream rails face the water. All eight water lanes take a boat or surfing partner underneath in either direction. A boat under the deck cannot climb sideways onto the road. On foot, crossing between the same two sills still takes nine steps. All 722 originally connected walking squares remain in the mainland walking circuit.

The separate pier by the cabin now has a closed timber end and short side rails painted onto its deck. Its existing Revive stays at the tip. South of the road the outlet bends east to a nine-square woodland landing. A wicker fishing creel on the bank holds a Lure Ball. Search it from the west or south. The landing can be reached only across the water. The path from the upstream channel mouth to the eastern shore is 19 paddle strokes.

Broad boards and a low timber rail carry the eye across the road. Piles meet the current under the deck, with thin ripples in open water and a lighter band along the bank. The cattails sit on the fishing shore. The added four rows of 8×8 art use four indexed shades and one native background palette per tile. The new metatiles reference 61 of those slots. All 133 earlier Kanto metatiles retain their original pixels.

Polished Crystal’s Olivine pier informed the spacing of the timber and Pokémon Orange’s Valencia Port informed the fishing landing. The road, water, construction and shore plants were drawn for Shiner Narrows.
