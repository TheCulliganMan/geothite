Fences: visible structural rails, fitted joinery, consistent sensible member
thickness, restrained aged wood grain, subtle edge variation and buried feet.
Preserve exact source fence runs, colors and exits. Uniform floating picket
strips fail. Source determines material identity; craft detail must not impose
brown wood on the white fences.

Signs: real thickness, crafted mount/frame, consistent glyph treatment and
material depth. Blank boards or random placeholder strokes fail. Generated
glyphs are aesthetic suggestions; production text/content comes from source.

Buildings: actual structural eaves/fascia/undersides, plausible small roof
courses, recessed doors/windows, quiet glass reflections, nuanced plaster/
masonry/timber and closed grounded foundations. Materials distinguish local
building identities. A good roof texture on an unfinished facade fails.

Ground: clear quiet walking areas, fine aggregate and subtle shallow wear,
low grass/litter at meaningful contacts. No smooth airbrushed empty sheets,
all-over giant cobbles, flower confetti or dressing that changes navigation.

Rock: broad meaningful fractured planes and restrained mineral strata,
resolved corner/contact geometry, continuous footing and readable ledges.
Padded uniform lumps, smooth concrete-like trainer platforms, exposed
undersides, floating ground strips and texture-only shape fixes fail.

Water: continuous coherent ripples, restrained reflections, shallow/deep color
relationship, damp grounded shore contact and source occupancy retained.
No rectangular glints, dry floating swimmers or invented bodies of water.
