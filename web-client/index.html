How to play

Move, explore, and meet other trainers.

D-pad
Move. Slide to change direction.
A
Talk, interact, confirm.
B
Go back.
START
Open the game menu.
2.5D camera

Drag the world left or right to orbit, up or down to zoom. Camera buttons are in Settings. Tap the percentage to reset.

Keyboard & controller

Arrow keys / WASD / left stick: move. Z / bottom face button: confirm. X / right face button: back. Space / Start: menu. Backspace / Back / View: Select. Right stick: orbit and zoom. On Steam Deck, use a Gamepad layout.

Online & chat

Face a trainer and press A to interact. Enter opens chat; Escape returns to the game.
